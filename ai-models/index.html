Independent catalogue of AI models for robotics. Compare what each developer states, field by field.

Independent by design — No paid listings. No affiliate links. No manufacturer rankings.

A robot AI model is software that helps a robot perceive, plan or act. A vision-language-action (VLA) model turns camera images and an instruction into movements; a world model predicts how a scene will change.
Models are not robots: when a developer names a robot that runs the model and that robot is in this catalogue, the entry links to its page.
