Notes

  • The producer calls Z01 a bipedal humanoid robot.
  • Z01 comes in an education version and a flagship version, according to the specification table (translated from the Chinese).
  • The producer's company page says Zvalley grew out of the construction machinery maker Zoomlion (translated from the Chinese).
  • The page lists uses in inspection rounds in workshops and data centres, guided tours in showrooms and museums, stage performances, and teaching and research (translated from the Chinese).
  • For guided tours the page names autonomous navigation and obstacle avoidance, with a map that is built once (translated from the Chinese).
  • The flagship version can be fitted with dexterous hands with 6 degrees of freedom each, according to the specification table.
  • The producer's site gives its address in the Xiangjiang New Area, Hunan, China.
