Notes

All-electric, enterprise-grade industrial humanoid robot built for material handling, part sequencing, machine tending, and order fulfillment.

Boston Dynamics frames this as an early-adopter/pilot-stage commercial product (first customer pilot cited: Hyundai, field testing on sequencing tasks); it supersedes the earlier hydraulic Atlas research platform, which is a different robot.

The specifications are from the sales sheet “Atlas-SalesSheet-12/23/2025”. The product page timeline calls the current robot the product version: “Our latest iteration of the Atlas robot is built for enterprise applications”.
