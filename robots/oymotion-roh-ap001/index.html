Notes

The datasheet gives a 12-month warranty: “12-month warranty period from the date of sale.”

OYMotion's ROHAND page calls the ROH-AP001 its newly launched second-generation dexterous hand (translated from Chinese).

Available for the left and right hand; versions with the suffix -C have a CAN interface.

The datasheet gives “Vertical distance from the tip of the middle finger to the wrist 183 mm”.

OYMotion's product page gives 0.7 seconds for a full finger stroke (translated from Chinese).

The datasheet gives a working environment of “temperature: -10 degrees Celsius ~ +40 degrees Celsius” and warns: “Warning: ROH-AP001 dexterous hand is not waterproof.”
