Notes

The producer describes RoBee F as combining two articulated arms, an autonomous omnidirectional base and a rear module for transport and storage.

The arms sit on a pneumatic lift with axial rotation for reaching several shelf levels.

The navigation base is listed with SIL 3 obstacle detection.

The producer gives its address as Via Galeazzo Viganò 7, 20841 Carate Brianza, Italy, with a US office in Hamilton, Ohio.
