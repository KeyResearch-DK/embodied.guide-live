Notes

X-VLA is a flow-matching vision-language-action model from the Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University, and Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory.

The Institute for AI Industry Research is in Tsinghua Science and Technology Park, Haidian District, Beijing.

The repository states that X-VLA has been accepted to ICLR 2026.
