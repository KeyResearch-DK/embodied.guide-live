Notes

  • Foundation's own site (foundation.bot) calls this robot simply “Phantom” (“Phantom is our first production humanoid robot, embodying decades of research and innovation in humanoid robotics.”). The “MK1”/“MK-1” designation used throughout industry press does not appear anywhere on the manufacturer's own Phantom page as checked 24 Sep 2026.
  • The city and the year of release come from press coverage, not from foundation.bot's own pages. Legal entity per the site's own copyright footer: “Foundation Future Industries, Inc.”
  • The homepage stat banner's PAYLOAD CAPACITY figure differs between the page's desktop layout (88.2lbs/40kg) and a narrower layout (20kg) of the same stat block on the same page. Height, weight, degrees of freedom and speed are the same in both layouts.
