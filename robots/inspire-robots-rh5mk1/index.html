Notes

Gasgoo reports that Inspire Robots at IROS 2026 (“held in Pittsburgh, U.S., from Sept. 27 to Oct. 1”) used the stage to “unveil its all-new RH5MK1 series, a 22-DOF fully direct-drive dexterous hand, for the first time anywhere in the world”.

Gasgoo describes the hand as one that “mirrors a human hand at a 1:1 scale”, with an “open-close frequency of 5Hz” and a “backdrivable torque of 0.05 N·m”.

Gasgoo reports: “The hand is fitted with 6D fingertip tactile sensors” offering “force resolution down to 0.005 N”. It adds that the hand relies on “self-developed high-strength joint modules with active zero-position calibration”.
