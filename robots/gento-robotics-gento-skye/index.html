Notes

The manufacturer's own products page lists Gento Skye as coming soon and gives no specifications.

A Chinese press report on Tianji's funding round (8 September 2026) says Tianji released the Gento humanoid brand with two wheeled humanoids, Luna and Skye, shown at ICRA and WAIC (translated from the Chinese).

The same report says delivery of the two robots has started (translated from the Chinese).

The specifications come from a reseller's product page (robotopian.com), not from the manufacturer's own site.

The reseller's specification table names the arm model as Marvin M6.

The press report gives the lifting range as 785 to 1,695 mm; the reseller's table gives 785 to 1,685 mm.

Intended use, per the manufacturer. “Aimed at reinforcement learning training and real-robot data collection (translated from the Chinese).”
