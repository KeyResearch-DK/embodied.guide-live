Notes

AGIBOT's own site lists A2-W as one of the A-series alongside A2 Ultra, A2 Lite and A3, described as a “Flexible Manufacturing Robot”.

A2-W moves on a four-wheel-drive chassis with a zero turning radius and crab-walking capability; it has no walking legs.

The page states a single-arm load of 5kg; this is an arm figure, not a whole-body payload.

AGIBOT's online store lists A2 Lite and A2 Ultra as purchasable products, but not A2-W, X1 or G2.
