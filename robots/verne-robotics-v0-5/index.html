Notes

Verne says V0.5 is the policy layer of a four-layer stack: a high-level reasoning layer, the policy layer, an execution layer and a controller.

Verne says V0.5 acts in end-effector space rather than joint space, so demonstrations from different robots share one description of motion.

Verne says it pretrained V0.5 on over 1.7 million demonstrations from data vendors, open-source datasets and its own collection.

Verne describes itself as a US company; its site footer names the company Verne Labs, Inc.
