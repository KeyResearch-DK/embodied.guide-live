Notes

UniX AI calls Panther its next-generation full-size wheeled dual-arm humanoid robot.

Each arm has 8 degrees of freedom and a reach of 982 mm.

The chassis has four-wheel steering and four-wheel drive, and moves in all directions.

The torso lifts through 600 to 800 mm.

The minimum passage width is 65 cm.
