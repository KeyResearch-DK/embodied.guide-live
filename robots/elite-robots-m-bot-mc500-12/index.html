Notes

Elite Robots describes the MC500-12 on its product page: “MC500-12 is an omnidirectional mobile manipulator combining a 300 kg-payload mobile platform with a 12 kg-payload collaborative robotic arm.”

The MC500-12 user manual, Ver1.0.0, is dated 10 June 2026 (“2026-06-10 Version: Ver1.0.0”). The manual describes the chassis navigation as “Based on laser SLAM navigation technology”.

A 12 October 2026 press release distributed by Elite Robots reports: “ELITEMISSION began the phased global rollout of four CE-compliant mobile robots on September 30”.
