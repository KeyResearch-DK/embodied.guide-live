Notes

AIDIN ROBOTICS showed the hand at AW 2026 in Seoul; its news page describes it as a product due for release this year (translated from the Korean).

The producer's LinkedIn post of 6 November 2026 describes the hand with “16 degrees of freedom” and does not separate the actively driven degrees of freedom from the total joint count.

AIDIN ROBOTICS' news page on the AW 2026 showing reproduces a trade-press report that says the payload was raised from 15 kg in the first generation to 20 kg (translated from the Korean). The grasp type and test conditions are not documented.

The producer's product page, which does not use the name Gen2, says: “Robot Hand Version 2 is scheduled for release in the second quarter of 2026”.

The producer's downloads page gives its address in “Dongan-gu, Anyang-si, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea”.
