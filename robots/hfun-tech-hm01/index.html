Notes

The producer calls HM01 a full-size general-purpose humanoid robot (translated from the Chinese).

The producer says HM01 uses its own full-size motion-control algorithm for stable straight-knee walking and dynamic balance (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's page says one battery lasts about 2 hours (translated from the Chinese).
