Notes

  • AGIBOT's product page (https://www.agibot.com/products/A2_Ultra) shares its product-parameters table with A2 Lite, presenting “A2 Ultra” and “A2 LITE” as columns.
  • Producer states A2 Ultra “has delivered rock-solid reliability and stability, proven through the deployment of over a thousand units in real-world operations”, with “up to 1300 hours of continuous walking without anomalies” in a stated 5100-hour walking test.
  • Producer states A2 Ultra “was the world's first humanoid robot to hold top-tier certifications across China, the US, and Europe (CR, CE-MD, CE-RED, FCC)”; “CR” names a different certification scheme than CCC/3C.
  • The table gives two runtime figures for A2 Ultra, “Standing: 3h” and “Walking: 1.5h+”.
  • PIA Automation offers a robot called P-BOT 2.0 whose datasheet gives the same weight (69 kg), height (169 cm), top speed (1.2 m/s) and charging time (2 hours), and PIA says its humanoid robots are made by Ningbo PIABOT Robotics, a joint venture with AGIBOT.
