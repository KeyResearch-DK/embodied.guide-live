Notes

AGIBOT's press release of July 18, 2026 says the company “today unveiled four new products at the World Artificial Intelligence Conference 2026 in Shanghai”.

AGIBOT's online store lists an OmniHand 3 Ultra with the text “OmniHand 3 Ultra Dexterous hand for research and data collection Contact sales”.

AGIBOT sells the hand family under the AGILINK brand: “AGILINK OmniHand & OmniPicker Dexterous hands and grippers for robotics.”

The brochure gives the dimensions as “Dimensions 189.5*88.5*40mm”.

AGIBOT states “a typical opening or closing time of 0.3 seconds and repeatability of ±0.2 millimeters”.

AGILINK states: “40–45 °C surface temperature under continuous load. 500K validated cycles”.
