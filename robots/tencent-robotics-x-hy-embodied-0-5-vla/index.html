Notes

  • Tencent Robotics X describes Hy-Embodied-0.5-VLA (short name Hy-VLA) as an end-to-end vision-language-action system built on the Hy-Embodied-0.5 backbone.
  • Two checkpoints are released: a pre-trained generalist one (Hy-VLA-UMI) and one post-trained on a simulation benchmark (Hy-VLA-RoboTwin).
  • Tencent's HY-Embodied repository also calls the release HY-VLA-0.5.
  • Tencent released the code, the checkpoints and more than 2,000 hours of two-armed demonstration data, recorded with a fingertip capture rig, on 15 June 2026.
