Notes

A lightweight open-source research quadruped from LAAS-CNRS, the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, and NYU.

Manufactured and sold by PAL Robotics in Barcelona; uses 12 torque-controlled brushless actuator modules with high impact compliance.

The initiative names its partners as the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems in Germany, New York University in the USA and LAAS-CNRS in France; it does not give one home country.
