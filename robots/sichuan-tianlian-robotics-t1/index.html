Notes

The producer calls T1 a bipedal humanoid robot (translated from the Chinese).

The specification table gives a maximum joint speed of 720 degrees per second, with a limit of 900, and peak torques of 450 N·m at the hip, 281 N·m at the knee and 148 N·m at the ankle.

The producer's site gives its headquarters in Mianyang High-tech Zone, Sichuan, China.
