Notes

  • The investor-relations record of Changan dates the meeting to 21 November 2025, at the Changan stand at the Guangzhou auto show (translated from the Chinese).
  • The record says the humanoid robot Xiaoan was demonstrated to the investors present, walking by itself, performing martial-arts and dance moves and answering by voice (translated from the Chinese).
  • Changan says Xiaoan can talk with people, demonstrate martial arts and help people with work, and that other functions are still being developed and tested (translated from the Chinese).
  • The record also says the company showed humanoid-robot products at its stand at the Guangzhou auto show (translated from the Chinese).
