Notes

The producer calls Jingling a wheeled robot with a two-wheel drive base (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's site is in Chinese only (its English language switch is disabled) and gives no English product name; the robot's name is a pinyin transliteration of the Chinese one.

The producer's photo shows a head, a torso and two arms with grippers on a column above a wheeled base.

The page says the robot navigates autonomously, guides visitors and recognises faces (translated from the Chinese).

The page says the whole-body motor control interface is open, with an SDK and documentation (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's contact page gives an address in Wuchang District, Wuhan, Hubei, China.
