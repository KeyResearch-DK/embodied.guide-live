Notes

IT Home calls Xingqing a heavy-duty general-purpose bipedal robot for industrial manufacturing, with a flexible high-precision torso (translated from the Chinese).

It comes with many preloaded industrial skills, according to IT Home (translated from the Chinese).

The robot's name is a pinyin transliteration of the Chinese one.

IT Home reported on 18 July 2026 that ZTE launched its Xing series of humanoid robots during the 2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference, with Xingqing among its industrial models (translated from the Chinese).
