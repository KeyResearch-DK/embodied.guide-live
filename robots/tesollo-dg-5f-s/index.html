Notes

Tesollo announced on 24 February 2026 that it had officially commercialized the hand.

The hand carries a one-year warranty from the delivery date.

Tesollo announced on 7 January 2026 that the prototype would be unveiled for the first time at CES 2026.

The DG-5F-S retains the core architecture and manipulation performance of Tesollo's flagship DG-5F-M.

Maximum current consumption: 10 A.
