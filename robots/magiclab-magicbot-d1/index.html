Notes

The producer calls MagicBot D1 a wheeled humanoid.

The chassis has four steering wheels and moves in any direction with zero-radius turning.

MagicLab's own case study says the robot is in use: “MagicBot D1 is already in active operation inside Dreame's smart manufacturing facility”.

The case study describes the work as in-shop material transport and line-side loading and unloading.
