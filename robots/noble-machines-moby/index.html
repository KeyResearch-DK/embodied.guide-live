Notes

The name Moby appears on the producer's blog post of May 12, 2026, which describes demonstrations “across multiple Moby robots” at NVIDIA GTC 2026. The home page shows the specifications without naming the model.

The privacy policy on the producer's site is issued by “Under Control Robotics Inc.” with a mailing address in Sunnyvale, California.

The home page offers “Limited RaaS pilots fully operated by our team, from deployment to daily execution”, and select platform access for teams that develop and test their own use cases.

The company page lists “First platform delivery” in October 2025 and says the company built and shipped “America’s first industrial humanoid robot”.

The home page says the robot is “Designed to handle stairs, scaffolding, unstable terrain, and cluttered spaces” and gives a walking speed of 0.8 m/s. The blog post refers to its “legged system”.
