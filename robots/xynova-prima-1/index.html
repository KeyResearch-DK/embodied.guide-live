Notes

Xynova's company page lists for August 2026: “Released Prima 1, a research-grade 22-DoF pure direct-drive dexterous hand, forming a dual-product strategy together with Flex 2.”

The documentation says the hand “uses direct drive at every joint and is designed for high-precision force-controlled manipulation and advanced embodied intelligence research”.

The documentation lists a hand size of 190 × 90.5 × 44 mm, a grip diameter range of 10 to 220 mm, a repeatability of 0.5 mm and a minimum backdrive force of 0.2 N.

Xynova's company page gives its address as “Floors 15-16, Building B2, Cloud Valley Center, No. 1009 Dengcai Street, Xihu District, Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China.”
