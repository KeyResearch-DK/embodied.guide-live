Notes

Xinhua reports that Vbot showed ATOM for the first time at its launch event at the 2026 World Robot Conference on 19 August and opened pre-orders (translated from the Chinese).

Xinhua describes ATOM as a humanoid embodied-intelligence terminal designed for living spaces (translated from the Chinese).

Xinhua names four kinds of space for ATOM: homes, retail, public services such as hotels, and offices (translated from the Chinese).

Xinhua says ATOM has a face screen with ear lights, a fabric-covered body and quiet foot soles (translated from the Chinese).

36kr reports that ATOM was launched in a personal version, an enterprise version and a developer version at the same time, with reservations opened at WRC.

The producer's website names the company Vbot (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd. (translated from the Chinese).
