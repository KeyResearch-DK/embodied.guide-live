Notes

  • Tesla Oracle reports that, on the Q3 2025 earnings call, Elon Musk said Tesla looked forward to unveiling Optimus V3, probably in Q1 2026.
  • Teslarati reports that on 31 March 2026 Musk said on X that Optimus 3 is walking around but needs some finishing touches before it is ready to be shown.
  • Tesla Oracle says Optimus V3 will also be a prototype and that Tesla's humanoid robot is still a few years away from going on sale to the public.
  • Not a Tesla App reports (31 March 2026) that the new hand design of this generation features 22 degrees of freedom; the article does not name who stated the figure.
  • Teslarati reports (23 September 2026) that design files in an Android version of the Tesla app appear to show Optimus Gen 3, with hands that Tesla has said carry 22 degrees of freedom, and that no reveal date has been announced.
  • The same Teslarati article says Musk later said Tesla would hold the design back until closer to production, partly to keep competitors from copying it.
