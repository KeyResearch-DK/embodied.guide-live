Notes

The producer's product manual calls the C2 a skin-type bipedal humanoid for culture and tourism, with two 6-DoF arms, human-like skin, dexterous hands and fine facial-expression actuation (translated from the Chinese).

The manual gives the face 26-34 DoF and at least 10 emotion expressions (translated from the Chinese).

The manual gives an arm-end load of about 3 kg (translated from the Chinese). That is an arm figure, not a figure for what the whole robot can carry.

The manual lists CAN bus and EtherCAT bus as communication methods (translated from the Chinese).

The manual lists unlimited-turn dialogue with a large language model as a feature (translated from the Chinese).
