Notes

Daimon Robotics launched the DM-Hand1 on 16 April 2025, together with the DM-Tac W vision-based tactile sensor and the DM-EXton teleoperation data collection system; the launch release is dated 17 April 2025 (translated from the Chinese).

The release describes the control: “With a hybrid force-position control algorithm, it can precisely mimic human hand movements to perform accurate grasping actions.”

The English launch release adds: “The product series are now available for global developers.”

The producer lists its Shenzhen office in the Bao'an district (translated from the Chinese).
