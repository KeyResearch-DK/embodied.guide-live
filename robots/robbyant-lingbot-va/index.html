What the data labels mean

Notes

  • Robbyant describes LingBot-VA as an autoregressive diffusion framework that learns frame prediction and policy execution simultaneously.
  • The model interleaves video and action tokens in one sequence: a dual-stream mixture-of-transformers design.
  • Robbyant released the weights and code for the shared-backbone version on 29 January 2026.
