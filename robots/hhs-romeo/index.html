Notes

The producer's site is in Chinese only.

The footer of the producer's page gives the company name as Shanghai HHS Robotics Co., Ltd. (translated from the Chinese).

The producer describes Romeo R1, the research edition, as a modular biped humanoid robot platform for research, teaching and applied development (translated from the Chinese).

The producer says Romeo R1 suits university research, service-robot development, assistive technology, interactive entertainment and industrial prototyping (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's page for the commercial edition titles it “HHS Nova B2”, a biped humanoid robot and commercial service platform (translated from the Chinese).

The producer lists interactive services such as reception, guiding, product demonstration and information enquiries in commercial spaces for the commercial edition (translated from the Chinese).
