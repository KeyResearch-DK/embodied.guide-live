Notes

The producer calls the SkyBot AR-H1 a commercial embodied humanoid robot with a parallel Stewart waist, bionic arms and an omnidirectional mobile chassis.

The chassis has dual steering wheels and a zero turning radius.

The page gives a maximum payload of 200 kg for the parallel waist.

The producer's Chinese site gives an address in Binhai New Area, Tianjin, China.
