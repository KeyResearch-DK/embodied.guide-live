Notes

Built by Boston Dynamics under DARPA's M3 program to explore bio-inspired high-speed running; ran only on an instrumented treadmill, tethered to an off-board hydraulic supply.

Set successive land-speed records for legged robots: 18 mph in March 2012, then 28.3 mph in September 2012 — faster than Usain Bolt's peak speed.
