Notes

  • DexForce's W1 Pro page shows two columns in its specification table, W1 Pro (full-body form factor) and W1 Core (upper-body form factor). The figures are from the W1 Pro column.
  • The W1 Pro moves on a wheeled chassis: the table lists the movement mode as “Differential (Optional: Omnidirectional)” and the page describes no walking gait. Its torso row gives “Leg: 2 DOF”.
  • Total degrees of freedom depend on the chassis: the page gives 34 for the differential chassis and 40 for the omnidirectional chassis.
