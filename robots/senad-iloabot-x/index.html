Notes

The producer's product page calls iLoabot-X a heavy-load humanoid robot focused on logistics scenarios.

The producer's product page describes it as an industrial-grade heavy-load embodied-intelligence robot for the rigid short-haul last 20 meters of industrial logistics.

The producer's news of 12 August 2025 presents iLoabot-X as a heavy-load dual-arm autonomous operation robot appearing for the first time at the World Robot Conference.

The producer's news of 27 September 2025 calls iLoabot-X the company's first heavy-load dual-arm humanoid-type robot.

The producer's news of 12 August 2025 describes the robot pushing a cage cart on its own.

The producer's news of 24 April 2026 says the end effectors can be exchanged, for example grippers and suction cups.

The CMRA news site reports that Senad showed the iLoabot-X 2.0, an upgraded iteration of iLoabot-X, during WAIC 2026.
