Notes

On 22 June 2026 Collaborative Robotics (Cobot) announced the second generation: “Collaborative Robotics Inc. (Cobot) today announced the second-generation Proxie, a general-purpose mobile collaborative robot”.

Cobot first launched Proxie publicly on 20 November 2024.

The Gen 2 announcement says: “The second-generation Proxie is available to order now starting at $5,000 per month.” It also states: “Proxie is in production today with enterprise customers across healthcare, manufacturing, and logistics”.

“Proxie Gen2 also introduces a modular option for two-armed interactions.” The Robot Report writes: “The company is currently using research-grade manipulators but expects to introduce industrial-hardened versions later this year.”
