Notes

  • The producer's site is in Chinese only and gives no English product name; the robot's name is a pinyin transliteration of the Chinese one.
  • The producer calls Jingang G1 the world's first dual-column wheeled humanoid robot (translated from the Chinese).
  • The producer's photo shows a head and two arms with grippers on a torso between two columns, above a wheeled base.
  • The page text calls the chassis a four-wheel drive with four-wheel steering (translated from the Chinese).
  • The specification table gives the chassis type as four-wheel differential (translated from the Chinese).
  • The table gives a ditch-crossing width of 100 mm.
  • The producer's site gives the company name as Suzhou Pangolin Robot Co., Ltd. (translated from the Chinese) and links an English name, Alpha Robotics Co., Ltd.
