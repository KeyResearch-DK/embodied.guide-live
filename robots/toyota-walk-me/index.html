Notes

Toyota says the Walk Me is one of three prototypes in its Me series and that they are still prototypes.

Toyota showed the Walk Me at the Japan Mobility Show 2025, which ran from 30 October to 9 November 2025.

Toyota says the Walk Me gets around on four legs instead of wheels, to navigate steps and uneven surfaces indoors and outdoors.

Toyota says the Walk Me learned its movements from some 10,000 motion simulations.

At the show the Walk Me stood up, walked and turned without a rider, controlled remotely; Toyota is considering rider controls such as an armrest joystick (translated from the Japanese).

A press report says it can fold into the size of a carry-on bag in thirty seconds at the touch of a button.
