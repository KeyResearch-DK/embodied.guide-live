Notes

Unitree describes G1+ as retaining the G1's value “with comprehensive upgrades in motion, perception, and intelligence”.

Unitree lists two editions, G1+ and G1+ EDU, in one specification table; the figures are for the G1+ column.

Unitree gives an “Arm Maximum Load” of Approx. 3kg for G1+ (Approx. 4kg for G1+ EDU) and says it “varies greatly under different arm extension postures”. This is a single-arm figure, not what the whole robot can carry.

TechNode (secondary source) reports that Unitree has launched the G1+ as an upgraded version of its G1 in an article dated 15 September 2026.

TechNode (secondary source) reports the standard G1+ as priced at RMB 95,000 in China; Unitree's own page gives US $15K excluding tax and shipping.
