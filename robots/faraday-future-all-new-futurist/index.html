Notes

  • The specification table has two columns, All-New Futurist and All-New Futurist Ultra. The figures are for All-New Futurist. The Ultra version has 51 DOF, LiDAR and a Jetson Thor-U and costs $129,900.
  • The page has an “Order Now” button and says “Start From $89,900”.
  • Faraday Future describes itself as “A California-based global shared intelligent-mobility ecosystem company. Founded 2014.” The page also links to a separate Chinese-language site, faradayfuturecn.com.
  • The page describes the motion control as “Steadier with every step and more coordinated in every motion.”
  • The page does not say which city Faraday Future is based in. The city comes from the company's filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, which give its principal executive offices in El Segundo, California.
  • Faraday Future's press release of 22 June 2026 announces the launch of the All-New Futurist at Automate in Chicago.
