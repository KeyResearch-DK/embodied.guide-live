Notes

The producer's hardware description for the Y2 series lists arms, a depth camera, a mobile chassis, a lift mechanism, grippers (customer option, not included by default), an optional voice module and a waist pitch joint (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's documentation menu for the Y2 series lists lift, head joint, waist joint, arm and gripper interfaces (translated from the Chinese).

The producer files the Y2-P under composite robots on its product page (translated from the Chinese).

The producer lists a working range of 2194 mm, a lift stroke of 700 mm and a turning radius of 406 mm (translated from the Chinese).

The producer lists its Sichuan head office in the Mianyang high-tech zone (translated from the Chinese).
