Notes

The Robot.com press release unveiling R-dog is dated 11 August 2026.

Robot.com says R-dog launched as a concept, with commercial deployments planned for 2027.

The trade association Automate reports that R-dog has four legs, each equipped with a wheel, and that Robot.com declined to share specifications because the system is still a prototype.

Robot.com says R-dog is designed to carry deliveries up and down stairs, over curbs and thresholds and across mixed terrain.

Robot.com says R-dog has a large onboard compartment for several items at once and hands over orders by unattended auto drop-off.

Robot.com says brands will be able to upload campaigns to R-dog's dual video screens.

Robot.com says it was founded in 2017 and is headquartered in San Francisco.
