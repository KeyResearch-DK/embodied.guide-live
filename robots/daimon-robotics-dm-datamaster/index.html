Notes

Presented by Daimon Robotics for “Delicate force/tactile feedback and efficient data acquisition”: “Integrated with Daimon's self-developed vision-based tactile sensors and haptic feedback technology, it achieves millinewton-level (mN) contact force difference feedback.”

Available in Backpack Version and Stand-Mounted Version (“Deploy your way: Backpack or Stand”).

Intended use, per the manufacturer. “Paired with full-axis high-precision encoder joints, it ensures high-quality data collection for high-sensitivity tasks (such as precision assembly and soft grasping).”
