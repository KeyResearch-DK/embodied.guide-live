Notes

Google calls Gemini 4 Argon its frontier model for complex workflows in software engineering, enterprise knowledge work and cybersecurity defense; its robotics post presents it as a general-purpose physical reasoning model.

Google made Gemini 4 Argon available to developers on 13 October 2026.

Google DeepMind's registered office (Companies House, company 07386350) is in London.
