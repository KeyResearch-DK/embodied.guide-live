Notes

  • The producer's product images show the robot with a wheel at the end of each leg, and the spec sheet heads the speed figure “Ready to roll”.
  • The producer gives its address as Hexagon Robotics GmbH, Räffelstrasse 26, 8045 Zurich, Switzerland.
  • At the launch in June 2025 the producer said Schaeffler and Pilatus would pilot AEON across a variety of use cases.
  • The launch announcement describes a battery swapping mechanism with which AEON does not need to recharge to continue to operate.
