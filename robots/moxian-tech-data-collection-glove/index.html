Notes

Moxian Tech markets the Tactile Data Collection Glove for dexterous manipulation training, teleoperation, and embodied AI data collection.

The product manual covers both the RigidCore (FPC, 842 tactile points) and Fabric (360 tactile points) variants paired with 5 VetraSense 6-DOF EMF sensors.
