Notes

Anyverse calls K15 a general-purpose humanoid robot.

Pedaily.cn calls K15 a general-purpose wheeled semi-humanoid robot for dexterous work (translated from the Chinese); the producer's page does not say how it moves.

The page lists smart manufacturing, the service industry and home service as application scenarios.

The first K15 units have been shipped to Europe, according to pedaily.cn (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's page gives an address in Haidian District, Beijing.
