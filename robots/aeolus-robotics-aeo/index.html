Notes

Aeolus does not publish numeric specifications for aeo's dimensions, weight, battery, or speed anywhere on its own site.

Aeolus states 7 degrees of freedom per arm and an 8 lb (3.6 kg) lift per arm; no whole-robot total is given.

aeo has a head, torso and two arms on a wheeled base, and no legs.

Aeolus does not state its location on its own site; country and city are taken from company registries (LinkedIn, CB Insights), which agree: “San Jose, California, United States”.

Named customers in Japan (HIMEDIC Inc., Globeship Corporation, Medical Care Service Inc.) describe aeo as already in operation there (Aeolus Robotics CES 2023 press release).

Robotics 24/7 reported on 4 January 2023 that Aeolus introduced aeo at CES 2023.
