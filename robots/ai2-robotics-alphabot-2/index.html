Notes

AI² Robotics' own English-language developer documentation (docs.ai2robotics.com/en/, a subdomain of the manufacturer's own domain) states plainly that “AlphaBot 2 is a wheeled dual-arm humanoid robot”; it also separately describes a three-joint folding lift structure with a waist yaw atop a two-wheel differential-drive chassis for torso-height adjustment — a lift mechanism, not walking legs.

Degrees of freedom (22) is stated as excluding the end effector (hand); a separate figure of 7 DoF is given per arm, and the page gives no separate hand-DoF count.

The page separately lists a chassis-mounted RGBD camera (qty. 1) alongside the body/arm camera set (3x Intel RealSense D435 on chest/head/back, 2x Intel RealSense D405 on the hands).

The chassis-parameter table also states a payload of up to 100kg for the mobile base itself; a base/chassis load rating is a different measurement from a walking or standing whole-body payload.
