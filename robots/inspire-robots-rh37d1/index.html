Notes

Gasgoo reported on 16 October 2026 that at the 2026 China International Industry Fair in Shanghai Inspire-Robots was “showcasing its all-new RH37D1 series three-finger dexterous hand”.

Gasgoo adds: “It integrates compliant control with multi-mode grasping capabilities, targeting industrial automation and smart manufacturing applications.”

Pandaily, which reported the launch on 15 October 2026, describes “The new product is a three-finger hand built for heavy industrial grasping rather than for imitating the human hand.”
