Notes

Wonik Robotics lists sales partners in China, in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, and in Taiwan for the Allegro Hand series.

Fingertip options are sold separately; type A is the default: “Various fingertip options provided (sold separately)”; “Type A (Default)”.

Gear ratio 288.35:1 / 576.7:1, stall torque 0.92 Nm / 1.84 Nm and nominal torque 0.23 Nm / 0.46 Nm, each pair followed on the page by the remark 2nd joint of the finger excluding the thumb; joint resolution 0.088 deg.

CE certified; attestation No. SZT-25MA37567-1, issued 24.02.2025.

The Allegro Hand FAQ says: “If you would like to purchase the Allegro Hand, please contact us through the Contact Us page on our website.” “We, Wonik Robotics, offer a one-year hardware warranty from the date of purchase.”
