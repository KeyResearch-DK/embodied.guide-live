Notes

The producer calls ugo Nova a Japanese-made semi-humanoid robot with two arms on four mecanum wheels (translated from the Japanese).

The joints are listed as head 2, each arm 7 plus a two-finger hand with 1, waist 4 and the wheeled base 4 (translated from the Japanese).

The producer gives an arm reach of 843 mm and a lifting stroke of 350 mm (translated from the Japanese).

The producer gives its address as Higashi-Kanda 1-7-8, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo (translated from the Japanese).
