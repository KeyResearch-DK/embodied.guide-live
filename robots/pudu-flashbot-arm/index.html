Notes

Pudu calls FlashBot Arm a semi-humanoid embodied AI service robot, developed by Pudu X-Lab.

It combines delivery with two arms and dexterous hands; it can press lift buttons, swipe access cards and open doors.

Each arm has 7 degrees of freedom; each hand has 6 active and 5 passive degrees of freedom.

The robot has an enclosed compartment with two doors.

The minimum path width is 65 cm.
