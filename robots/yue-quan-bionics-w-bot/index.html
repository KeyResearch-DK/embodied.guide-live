Notes

China Youth Daily, republished by China's national science and technology news site, reports that the W-Bot appeared alongside the X-Bot at the 2025 Bionic Robotics and Bionic Intelligence Conference in Beijing (translated from the Chinese).

The same report names the conference as the 2025 Bionic Robotics and Bionic Intelligence Conference, held at Future Science City in Changping, Beijing (translated from the Chinese).

According to 36Kr, the company's production base located in Changping, Beijing is under construction.
