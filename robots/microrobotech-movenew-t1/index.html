Notes

The manufacturer has rebranded: its old domain now redirects to a new one, and the current site uses a different brand name.

The manufacturer's own label, “Total Battery Weight”, is ambiguous — it could mean the whole robot or just the battery. It sits grouped with dimensions, not electrical specs.

No price is published on either language version of the manufacturer's site — only “Buy Now” and “Get Solutions” contact buttons, no figure.
