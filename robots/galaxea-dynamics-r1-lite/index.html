Notes

Galaxea Dynamics' own site presents R1 Lite alongside two sibling configurations, R1 and R1 Pro, in a single comparison table; the figures given are for the R1 Lite column only.

R1 Lite moves on the same wheeled chassis as its R1 and R1 Pro siblings, with 3 steering wheels (chassis DOF 6, chassis speed 1.5 m/s); it has no walking legs.

R1 Lite's torso lift gives a vertical workspace of 0-1.7 m, versus 0-2 m for the R1 and R1 Pro configurations, and its single-arm mount is “top or side-mounted” rather than the side-only mount used on R1 and R1 Pro.

R1 Lite carries a single stereo (binocular) camera as its whole sensor suite, unlike R1 and R1 Pro, which each add 7 HD cameras and a LiDAR unit.

The arm carries a rated payload of 2 kg (maximum 3.5 kg) at 0.5 m reach, the same figure as the base R1 configuration; this is an arm figure, not a whole-body payload.

The company's registered name on this page reads, in English, “Galaxea (Beijing) Artificial Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd.” (the Chinese original is in the source wording).
