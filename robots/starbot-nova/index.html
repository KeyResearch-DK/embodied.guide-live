Notes

StarBot describes itself as a Santa Barbara robotics company building embodied-AI platforms for education, university research and real-world service operations.

StarBot dates the launch of Nova to December 2025 and describes it as a wheeled base with two 7-DOF arms.

StarBot calls Nova its first-generation humanoid service robot and an interface for supervised voice, navigation and service-workflow research.

The specification sheet lists customer greeting, store navigation and guidance, product and service explanation, and queue management as functions.

The specification sheet lists English, Spanish and Italian as voice languages, customizable.

StarBot says the specification sheet describes a Nova reference configuration that may vary by model, options, software release and operating environment.
