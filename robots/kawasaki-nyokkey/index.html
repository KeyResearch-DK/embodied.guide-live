Notes

Kawasaki calls Nyokkey an autonomous mobile type service robot that works with two arms.

The producer's photo shows a screen face on a neck, a torso with two arms and a four-wheeled base.

Kawasaki began developing Nyokkey in spring 2021 as a patrol robot watching over patients in a hospital.

Kawasaki has since developed Nyokkey further for a wide range of uses in the service industry.

The producer describes Nyokkey as a platform: Kawasaki builds the hardware and develops the software for each use with partners.

The page names uses such as serving meals and clearing dishes in a restaurant, and patrolling and supporting cleaning work in buildings.

A remote collaboration system, Successor, lets an operator take over by remote control when a task is hard to automate or an error occurs, according to the producer.
