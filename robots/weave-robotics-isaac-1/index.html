Notes

Weave Robotics also makes a separate, stationary laundry-folding robot called Isaac 0, already shipping. This is the newer, mobile Isaac 1 on a wheeled base, which the company describes as its “most capable home robot yet.”

Degrees of freedom are given only per body part on the producer's own page: Neck 2, Arms 2 x 6, Hands 2 x 1, Torso 2, Base 3 - never as a single total for the robot.

Stated reach: 80 in (203 cm) vertical, 38 in (97 cm) horizontal.

No weight, whole-body payload, top speed, slope rating, sensor details (camera/lidar type or count), onboard compute, ROS2/SDK support, or certifications are stated anywhere on the producer's own site.

Isaac 1 has no legs; it moves on a wheeled base with a height-adjustable torso.
