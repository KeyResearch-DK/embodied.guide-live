Max speed8.64km/h4.9mphThe manufacturer’s own imperial figure.
Source and note: Max speedA ghostrobotics.io/vision-60 · Retrieved 19 August 2026Caveat. Two errors on the manufacturer's own page. (1) The bullet list at the top of the product page states 'up to 2.5 meters/second (5.6 miles/hour)'; the specification table further down the same page states 2.4 m/s as sprint. (2) The pair 2.4m/s (4.9 mph) does not check out: 2.4 m/s = 5.37 mph, and 4.9 mph = 2.19 m/s. The other pairs on the line are correct (0.9 = 2.01; 1.2 = 2.68; 3.0 = 6.71); only the sprint pair is wrong, on both the product page and the datasheet. Standard walk is 0.9 m/s (2 mph), fast-walk 1.2 m/s.
