Notes

  • The Octo repository describes Octo models as transformer-based diffusion policies trained on a diverse mix of 800k robot trajectories.
  • The Octo paper was first submitted to arXiv on 20 May 2024.
  • The UC Berkeley admissions page gives its address as Berkeley, CA.
