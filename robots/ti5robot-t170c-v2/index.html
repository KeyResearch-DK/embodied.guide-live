Notes

The producer calls the T170C V2.0 a general-purpose wheeled humanoid robot for industrial, commercial, entertainment and research settings (translated from the Chinese).

The chassis uses two-wheel differential drive (translated from the Chinese).

The legs have a folding design with 2 degrees of freedom (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's site gives an address in Minhang District, Shanghai, China.
