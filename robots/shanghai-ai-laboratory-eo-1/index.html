EO-1

China · 2025

Runs on G1 by AgibotSource and note: G1B arxiv.org/html/2508.21112 · Retrieved 15 October 2026

Sources retrieved: 15 October 2026 (A, B, C) · 16 October 2026 (D)

Model type
Vision-language-action (VLA) Embodied reasoningSource and note: Model typeA huggingface.co/IPEC-COMMUNITY/EO-1-3B · Retrieved 15 October 2026Note. Model card: “EO-1 model is a Vision-Language-Action (VLA) model that adopts a single unified decoder-only transformer”; it is built “for multimodal embodied reasoning and robot control, enabling seamless perception, planning, reasoning, and acting in single model”.
What it controls
Arms and handsSource and note: What it controlsA huggingface.co/IPEC-COMMUNITY/EO-1-3B · Retrieved 15 October 2026Note. Model card, Output: “Output Type(s): Actions, Language”. The real-robot tests are arm and dual-arm manipulation tasks: “Agibot G-1 Long-Horizon Dexterity (4 Tasks).”
Inputs
Camera Language Robot stateSource and note: InputsA huggingface.co/IPEC-COMMUNITY/EO-1-3B · Retrieved 15 October 2026Note. Model card, Input Type: “Vision: Image Frames, Video”; “State: Robot Proprioception”; “Language Instruction: Text, Pointing, Bounding Box, etc.”. Image frames and video = camera, robot proprioception = robot state.
Access
Open weights (download)Source and note: AccessC eo-robotics.ai/eo-1 · Retrieved 15 October 2026Note. Project page: “EO-1 is released with full openness, including model weights, training code, and all components of the interleaved embodied dataset”.
Parameters
3BSource and note: ParametersA huggingface.co/IPEC-COMMUNITY/EO-1-3B · Retrieved 15 October 2026Note. Model card: “an open-source unified embodied foundation model comprising 3B parameters”.
Base model
Qwen2.5-VL-3B-InstructSource and note: Base modelA huggingface.co/IPEC-COMMUNITY/EO-1-3B · Retrieved 15 October 2026Note. Model card: the tokens are processed by the “shared transformer backbone, whose weights are initialized from Qwen2.5-VL”; base model listed as “Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct”.

Notes

The EO Robotics team at Shanghai AI Laboratory describes EO-1 as an open-source unified embodied foundation model with 3B parameters.

The EO-1 paper was first submitted to arXiv on 28 August 2025.

EO Robotics is part of the Intelligent Photonics and Electronics Center of Shanghai AI Laboratory in Xuhui, Shanghai.

The EO Robotics website is copyrighted by Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory.
