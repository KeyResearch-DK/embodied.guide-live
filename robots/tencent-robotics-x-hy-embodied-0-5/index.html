The developer has released this model in several versions. Each figure applies to the version its source names.

Notes

Tencent Robotics X and the HY Vision Team describe HY-Embodied-0.5 as a suite of foundation models for embodied intelligence, built for spatial-temporal perception and embodied reasoning.

The family has a 2B edge model and a 32B model; the open weights are the MoT-2B model.

HY-Embodied-0.5-X is an extended variant of HY-Embodied-0.5 with additional post-training, released on 24 April 2026 with its own weights and licence file.

Tencent says HY-Embodied-0.5 can act as the language-and-vision part of a vision-language-action model.
