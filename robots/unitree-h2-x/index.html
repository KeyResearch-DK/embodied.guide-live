Notes

Unitree introduced the H2-X “Kung Fu Legend Guardian” in a post on its official TikTok account on 21 October 2026.

Global Times reports that Unitree unveiled the H2-X on Wednesday, 21 October 2026, with security and rescue scenarios shown in the official demonstration video.

Global Times reports that Unitree said the robot has a maximum operating time of about eight hours, compared with approximately three hours for the standard H2.

Interesting Engineering reports that Unitree said the H2-X can stand for eight hours.

Interesting Engineering reports that the H2-X builds on the existing H2 humanoid platform.

Unitree lists H2-X and H2-X EDU side by side on its product page. H2-X EDU adds dexterous-hand options, a PC2 (Intel Core i7 or Orin NX), an optional PC3 (Thor T5000 or Orin AGX), an optional RGBD depth camera in the chest, secondary development and a 12-month warranty against 8 months for H2-X.
