Notes

Sharpa's news item of 19 December 2025 says: “Sharpa has achieved a major milestone with the launch of mass production for SharpaWave”. It adds: “Early customers include leading global technology companies and top research universities.”

Sharpa's news page lists: “Sharpa Wins CES 2026 Innovation Award for Groundbreak AI Robotic Hand SharpaWave Company News: December 16,2025”.

The specification table reads: “Dimensions 208 mm × 90 mm × 50 mm”, “Minimum Grasp Diameter 10 mm”, “Grip Force 150 N 90 N”, “Fingertip Position Repeatability ±1 mm”, “Operating Temperature Range 0°C to 45°C” and “Rated Lifetime Over 1,000,000 cycles”.

The tactile sensor is listed with “Tactile Resolution 240×240 60×60” and “Frame Rate 180 fps 30 fps”. The press release says each fingertip captures contact “with 0.005 N precision”.

The site footer reads “© Sharpa Pte Ltd. All rights reserved.” A Sharpa news release says: “Sharpa is scaling its operations in its global headquarters of Singapore”.
