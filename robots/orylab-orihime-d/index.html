Notes

The producer describes OriHime-D as an avatar robot operated over the Internet that can move forward, backward and turn, with 14 joint motors in the upper body.

The photos on the Japanese product page show a head, a torso and two arms on a floor-standing base.

The Japanese product page says the product is currently not sold and is available for rental only (translated from the Japanese).

The Japanese product page says OriHime-D was developed with the cooperation of the Nippon Foundation and ANA AVATAR (translated from the Japanese).

The producer says it is looking for companies and research institutes for joint business development and research on OriHime-D.

The producer's English page gives an address in Chuo-ku, Tokyo, and the company name OryLab Inc.

The Japanese product page says the first avatar robot cafe, “DAWN ver.β”, opened on 26 November 2018 and shows photos of it (translated from the Japanese).
