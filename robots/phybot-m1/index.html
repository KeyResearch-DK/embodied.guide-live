Notes

  • The page footer gives the company name as Beijing Phybot Technology Co., Ltd.
  • The producer describes PHYBOT M1 as a high-performance, general-purpose humanoid robot developed by PHYBOT.
  • The parameter table gives the standing size as 1760×516×228 mm, the arm length as 575 mm and the leg and foot length as 925 mm.
  • The page lists a dual-arm payload of 10-20 kg and a backpack payload of at least 50 kg.
  • A PEdaily press report (29 July 2025) on the World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC 2025, Shanghai, 26-29 July 2025) says PHYBOT showed its PHYBOT M1 and PHYBOT C1 there (translated from the Chinese).
