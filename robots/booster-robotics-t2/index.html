Notes

Two configurations are sold, Education and Professional; the only difference on the producer's own page is the onboard compute chip. All other figures are shared.

The producer's own page breaks the total of 31 degrees of freedom down as leg 6, arm 7, waist 3, head 2.

The stated “Maximum Dual-Arm Payload: 10kg” is the combined capacity of both arms, not a whole-body payload, and varies “significantly depending on arm posture” according to the producer's own footnote.

A maximum actuator torque of 140 N.m, industrial-grade crossed-roller joint bearings, and a high-speed internal-rotor PMSM motor are also stated.

Also stated: a screen is supported, low-battery and joint-overtemperature voice warnings are given, cooling is by local air cooling, and charging is via a magnetic port (no self-docking station is described).

Education and Professional configurations are sold, differing only in onboard compute (“High-performance ARM processor” vs. “Jetson Thor T5000”). The figures apply to both unless noted.

Booster Robotics' press release of 13 July 2026 (Newsfile) says the company officially launched Booster T2.
