Notes

The producer calls the Sherpa Mecha an industrial humanoid robot: two arms and an adjustable-height torso on an autonomous wheeled base instead of legs.

The producer's product list gives the robot's status as “Announced”.

The producer says it was founded in Bengaluru, India.

The producer's brochure gives an address in Rochester Hills, Michigan, USA, and its company page calls it the US headquarters.

The arms are built from carbon fibre, and the arm reach is given as 35 in (900 mm).
