Notes

Hitachi and Hitachi Building Systems announced in Tokyo on 26 March 2020 that they would launch full-scale commercial operations with the EMIEW communication robot.

Hitachi calls EMIEW4 a humanoid robot and the new model in the EMIEW communication robot lineup.

The producer says the EMIEW robot supports buildings with visitor reception, guidance and surveillance patrols.

Hitachi lists autonomous operation over a wide area, multilingual voice conversation and automated recharging for continuous operation without battery changes as the features of EMIEW4.

Hitachi gives sales in Japan from 20 April 2020, primarily to offices, hospitals and care facilities.

Hitachi gives the service price of EMIEW4 as an individual quotation and the standard delivery period as about four months after receipt of an order.

EMIEW4 supports voice conversation in Japanese, English, Chinese and Korean.
