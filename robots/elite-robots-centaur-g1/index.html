Notes

The producer's site is in Chinese only.

The producer presents Centaur-G1 as a wheeled humanoid robot (translated from the Chinese).

The producer says the robot uses an omnidirectional mobile base (translated from the Chinese).

The producer gives the robot a working radius of 2,200 mm and an operating height of 1,500 mm (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's contact page lists its headquarters base in the Suzhou Industrial Park (translated from the Chinese).
