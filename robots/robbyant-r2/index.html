Notes

Robbyant's own product page for R2 is marked “Coming Soon”; the figures below are the ones the producer nonetheless states on that page.

Robbyant describes itself as “an embodied AI company under Ant Group, building one brain for all robots”; its registered entity is Shanghai Ant Robbyant Technology Co., Ltd.

R2 moves on a compact chassis with omnidirectional steering wheels; it has no walking legs.

The page states a rated payload of 6 kg per arm, reaching from the floor to 2 m high; no whole-body payload figure is given.

The page also states joint torque feedback, six-axis force sensing at the end effector, and core joint brakes that lock on emergency stop.
