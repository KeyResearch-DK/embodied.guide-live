Notes

According to scmp.com, R1 is a wheeled humanoid with two arms from Robbyant, the robotics unit of Ant Group.

According to scmp.com, Robbyant says R1 is in production and has been shipped to customers, including a museum.

According to scmp.com, the company says R1 can work as a chef or tour guide, or perform remote-controlled tasks.

The producer's homepage lists KUAVO 4 Pro and Robbyant R2 as robot platforms and does not mention R1.
