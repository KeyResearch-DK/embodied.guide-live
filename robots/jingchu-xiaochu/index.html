Notes

The producer calls Xiaochu a small wheeled robot aimed mainly at cultural tourism, entertainment and home companionship (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's site is in Chinese only (its English language switch is disabled) and gives no English product name; the robot's name is a pinyin transliteration of the Chinese one.

The producer's photo shows a head, a torso, two arms with hands and two legs that end in wheels.

The page says the robot can carry an AI voice kit and five-fingered dexterous hands (translated from the Chinese).

A customised version with its own exterior is offered (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's contact page gives an address in Wuchang District, Wuhan, Hubei, China.
