Notes

Muks Robotics calls Spaceo PRO an enterprise humanoid and says it is available for pilots.

The chassis height is adjustable and the waist rotates, giving a working range from 0 to 145 cm.

Each arm carries up to 5 kg; the hands have 1 to 16 DoF depending on the gripper.
