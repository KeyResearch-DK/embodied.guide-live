Notes

The WRC exhibitor page says the company was founded on 29 October 2025 and is based in Linping district, Hangzhou (translated from the Chinese).

The WRC exhibitor page shows the robot working with a template machine to demonstrate a full autonomous sewing process (translated from the Chinese).

Hangzhou Net reports on 25 August 2026 that Aitu is a female humanoid robot designed for garment manufacturing (translated from the Chinese).

Hangzhou Net says the company chose a two- or three-finger gripper on a wheeled base (translated from the Chinese).

Hangzhou Net says the R1 is planned for official release on 28 October 2026 (translated from the Chinese).
