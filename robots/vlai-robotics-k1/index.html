Notes

The CMRA news site, 4 September 2026, reports that VLAI Robotics released the K1, a dual-arm humanoid robot with mobile dexterous manipulation, on 13 August.

The same report says the K1 has four wheels for omnidirectional movement and fully internal wiring.

ITBear, 23 January 2025, gives the company behind VLAI as a Guangzhou company whose Chinese name translates as Future Power Co., Ltd. (translated from the Chinese).
