Notes

Robot.com calls R-noid its flagship full-body humanoid, 1.7 m tall, with two arms and omnidirectional movement.

The specification sheet gives a 0.55 m wheel base.

The producer lists Astribot among its partners for R-noid, alongside NVIDIA, FieldAI, Formic, Pi and Yukai Engineering.

The producer's site gives no address; company registers (Crunchbase, CB Insights, PitchBook) give San Francisco, USA, for Robot.com, formerly Kiwibot.
