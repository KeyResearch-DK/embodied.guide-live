Notes

AeiROBOT calls AIMY a welcome robot that moves through indoor spaces on its own, greets customers and gives information.

The producer says AIMY makes eye contact and generates listening gestures while it talks, and can use a large language model such as ChatGPT.

The 6-DoF hands are an option.
