Notes

The specifications come from EncoSmart's press release of 16 April 2026, as published by the news outlet MyDrivers (translated from the Chinese).

EncoSmart presented Nico at the 139th China Import and Export Fair (Canton Fair, spring session) in Guangzhou on 16 April 2026 (translated from the Chinese).

EncoSmart's site says the company was founded in Beijing in May 2022 (translated from the Chinese).

The press release says Nico combines EncoSmart's own arm-manipulation technology with the all-terrain mobility of Ninebot (translated from the Chinese).

The press release describes Nico as a humanoid solution for cross-scene service in food service, from the back kitchen to the front of house, with 12 categories of service capability (translated from the Chinese).
