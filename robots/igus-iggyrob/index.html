Notes

igus calls iggyRob a humanoid robot: two ReBeL cobot arms on a ReBeLMove Pro mobile base, with a virtual face on a display.

The description names bionic hands; the product information lists one suction pad with CobotPump and one ReBeL gripper.

The producer sells iggyRob in its own online shop, with a listed price and an add-to-cart button.

The producer gives the ReBeLMove Pro base a load capacity of 50 kg and a payload of up to 100 kg.
