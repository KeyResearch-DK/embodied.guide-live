Notes

Xiaomi calls CyberOne a full-size humanoid bionic robot, developed in full by the Xiaomi Robotics Lab.

The producer says the vision system reconstructs the real world in 3D, with depth information accurate to 1% within 8 m.

According to 80.lv, CyberOne can recognize 85 types of environmental sounds and 45 classifications of human emotion.

According to 80.lv, the arm span is 168 cm.

Telco Magazine reported on 15 August 2022 that Xiaomi unveiled its first humanoid robot, CyberOne, at a launch event in Beijing.
