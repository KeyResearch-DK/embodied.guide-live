Notes

Unitree calls the R1 family a dual-arm humanoid robot.

According to CNX Software, the -D variants have a wheeled base with a LiDAR.

R1-A5-D has 5-DOF arms and R1-A7-D has 7-DOF arms; both have a 1-DOF waist, a 2-DOF head and a 3-DOF chassis, according to the specification table.

The arms take two-finger grippers or three- or five-finger dexterous hands.
