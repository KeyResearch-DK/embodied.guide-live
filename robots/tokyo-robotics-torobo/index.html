Notes

Tokyo Robotics lists Torobo on its own page as a humanoid robot that is under development.

The joint configuration is 7-axis dual arms, a 3-axis waist, a 3-axis neck and a 4-axis undercarriage that is an omni-directional mobile base.

Tokyo Robotics dates Torobo to 2022 on its page of earlier products, where it describes Torobo as a robot developed to accelerate industrial applications of full-body humanoid robots.

The page says that its videos show the old Torobo and that the current Torobo is lighter than the older version.
