Notes

Bumi is a compact consumer humanoid for family companionship, maker education and programming learning. The specification table lists Lite and Pro, and the EDU versions EDU-Air, EDU-Pro and EDU-Max.

Noetix says it has launched Bumi at a price under 10,000 CNY.

KrASIA reported on 24 October 2025 that Noetix Robotics unveiled the humanoid Bumi and opened preorders.

Noetix Robotics' After-Sales Terms apply to robots “sold by Noetix Robotics within mainland China” and give Bumi a 12-month warranty from initial device activation.
