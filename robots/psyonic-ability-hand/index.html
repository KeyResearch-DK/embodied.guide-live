Notes

PSYONIC also offers the Ability Hand as a prosthetic hand for people: “The Ability Hand has integrated sensors that detect pressure when you grip an object and sends a vibration to your arm to communicate the sensation.”

The spec sheet gives a length of “Large 186 mm (L)” or “Small 180 mm (L)” and a width of “85 mm (W)”.

The spec sheet gives water and dust ingress protection IP64 and warns: “The wrist must not be exposed to water at any time, as this can damage the batteries or internal components.”

The spec sheet gives a finger closing speed of 0.2 s and a maximum speed of 462 °/s for the fingers and 1848 °/s for the thumb.
