Notes

The producer describes Feather as a bimanual mobile robot: two 7-DoF arms on a torso that raises and lowers 600 mm, mounted on a holonomic base.

The producer calls its 1-metre arm reach best-in-class for a humanoid robot.

The producer says it is fulfilling orders now.

The producer describes itself as an American company.
