Notes

Guohua Intelligent's company brochure for the World Robot Conference 2026 calls Yunyi No. 2 the company's first general-purpose biped robot body (translated from the Chinese).

The brochure says Yunyi No. 2 helps customers with different research topics or rich industry scenarios (translated from the Chinese).

The brochure says Yunyi No. 2 can perform predefined actions such as walking, squatting and grasping (translated from the Chinese).

The brochure says Yunyi No. 2 carries voice-interaction hardware and can be controlled by voice (translated from the Chinese).

Guohua Intelligent's exhibitor page for the World Robot Conference 2026 says the company was founded in 2014 and is headquartered in Qingdao, Shandong (translated from the Chinese).

The exhibitor page lists wheeled and biped humanoid robot bodies among the company's core products (translated from the Chinese).
