Notes

Source: https://www.agibot.com/products/G1. This is AGIBOT's own “G1”, unrelated to Unitree's G1.

G1 moves on a wheeled chassis: body copy states “The chassis allows in-place turning, navigates 95% of pathways in factories, and mounts obstacles up to 20 mm in height” — no walking-leg or gait description appears anywhere on the page.

The page's own spec table gives “DoF (excluding end-effector): 20 DoF”, while body copy separately states “The robot features 26 DOFs”; the two are consistent (20 body DOF plus a separately stated 6-DOF end-effector = 26), not a contradiction.

Height is given as a range, “130~180cm”, not a single figure.

Producer states, under “Stable Operation and Mass Production”: “Large-scale production with immediate delivery”.

Body copy states “continuous operation while holding a 3 kg object with one arm” at “working height over 2 meters” — an arm/task figure, not a whole-body payload.

The page also separately describes G1 as a “General purpose robot designed for data collection and model inference”, alongside “Multi-Scenario Operations: Suitable for industrial, commercial, and domestic scenarios”.

PIA Automation offers a robot called I-BOT 1.0 whose datasheet gives the same weight (150 kg), height range (130-180 cm) and runtime (4+ hours), and PIA says its humanoid robots are made by Ningbo PIABOT Robotics, a joint venture with AGIBOT.
