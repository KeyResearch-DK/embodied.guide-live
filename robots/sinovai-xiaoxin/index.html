Notes

The producer's exhibitor page at the World Robot Conference 2026 gives 44 degrees of freedom, 7-axis arms, an operating height above 2 m and an 11-DoF dexterous hand, and says the robot can squat and raise or lower itself (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's product image on its exhibitor page shows a head, a torso and two arms with hands on a column above a wheeled base.

The producer says it is meant for cultural-tourism guiding, commercial service, research and education, and corporate reception (translated from the Chinese).

Science and Technology Daily of 20 September 2026 reports that Sinovai's embodied robot Xiaoxin welcomed visitors at the World Public Science Literacy Promotion Conference in Beijing (translated from the Chinese).
