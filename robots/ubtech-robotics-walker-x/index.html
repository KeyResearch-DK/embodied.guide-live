Notes

Built-in visual and 3D navigation (U-SLAM) with first-person-view AR navigation and 2.5D obstacle avoidance, plus object and facial recognition; not described as teleoperated.

Per IEEE Spectrum's Video Friday of 9 July 2021, UBTECH unveiled Walker X, the latest version of its bipedal humanoid.
