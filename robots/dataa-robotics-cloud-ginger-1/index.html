Notes

Dataa Robotics calls the robot the Cloud Ginger Elegant Humanoid Service Robot XR1, a cloud intelligent service robot based on Smart Compliant Actuators (SCA).

The robot stands on a three-wheeled chassis base with universal wheels.

The producer says the robot is competent for reception, guidance, performance, business processing, care and education services for clients in many fields.

The Chinese version of the product page gives the height as 160 cm and the weight as 65 kg (translated from the Chinese).

Dataa Robotics says it is headquartered in Shanghai, with branches in Beijing, Shenzhen, Chengdu and Zhuhai.

Dataa Robotics' milestones page groups the world debut of the Ginger XR-1 under 2018-2019 without giving a single year.
