Notes

PAL Robotics lists TIAGo Pro with two 7-DoF arms, an expressive pan-tilt head, a lifting torso and an omnidirectional base on four mecanum wheels.

The page notes that a single-arm version is also available.

The TIAGo Pro page carries a “Request a quote” button.

The product page gives a vertical arm reach of 92 cm; the 2024 datasheet gives an arm reach of 96 cm.
