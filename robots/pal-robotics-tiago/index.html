Notes

PAL Robotics offers TIAGo with no arm, one arm or two arms; each arm has 7 DoF.

The datasheet lists three versions, LITE, TIAGo and ++, and says upgrade kits are available to move from one version to another.

The base has differential drive as standard; omnidirectional drive is an option.

The producer says TIAGo has been on the market for over 9 years, and the page carries a configurator with a “Get a Quote” button.

The datasheet gives an arm reach of 87 cm without end-effector and a torso lift of 35 cm.
