Notes

UBTECH's own navigation groups Cruzr S2 under “Wheeled” (alongside Cruzr Y1), distinct from the “Industrial” and “Commercial” bipedal Walker family; the page itself calls it a “Full-sized Wheeled Humanoid Robot” with a “symmetrical body design with bidirectional bending function” on a mobile base, not legs.

Cruzr S2 can rotate +/-170 degrees within a 0.8m passage, and its 0-40cm torso lifting range lets it handle a 15kg payload across a 0-1.8m vertical workspace.

Sensor and port layout is shown as labels on the producer's component diagram: fish-eye camera x2, RGB camera x2, LED screen x1, microphone x1, speaker x2, RGBD x2 (chest and base), radar x1 (chest) + radar x1 (base), six-axis force sensor x2, dexterous hands x2, a power switch, an emergency-stop button, a Gigabit Ethernet/USB3.0 port, and a fast-charging port.

The dexterous hands and six-axis force sensors are built-in end effectors shown on the same diagram; they are not a mounting interface for third-party payloads.

No dimensions, weight, degrees of freedom, battery capacity, runtime, IP rating, onboard compute chip, ROS2/SDK support, or certifications are stated anywhere on the producer's own page.

No named autonomy level is given. The page describes a sim-to-real embodied intelligent data system and UBTECH's own “Co-Agent”; not described as teleoperated.
