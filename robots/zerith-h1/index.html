Notes

The producer describes the H1 series as a “wheel-arm humanoid robot” (translated from the Chinese): a humanoid upper body on a differential-drive wheeled base with a lifting lower section of 0-800 mm.

The specification table on the H1 page has a single column headed ZerithH1. The producer's solution pages also name an “H1 Pro” body for its robot shop and unmanned warehouse; the table does not mention a Pro version.

The parameters page tells buyers to confirm the appearance and configuration with the producer's sales staff before purchase (translated from the Chinese). No price and no shop are shown; enquiries go through a business-cooperation contact form.

The producer's site names the company as Hefei Zerith Robotics Co., Ltd. (translated from the Chinese).
