Notes

  • The product page says 4NE1 comes in “dedicated models for different environments”, with “versions on wheels for industrial floors or with a 7th axis for extended reach”.
  • The reservation page is for 4NE1 Gen 3.5 and says: “4NE1 Gen 3.5 is expected to be available end of 2026.” A refundable reservation fee of 100 EUR per unit secures a place in the delivery queue.
  • The datasheet of June 2026 gives a speed of 5 km/h and says that “Leg-supported configurations can carry up to 100 kg”.
  • NEURA publishes two datasheets for 4NE1. The one linked from the reservation page is for Gen 3.5 (V6, 2 January 2026); the one linked from the product page (V7, 5 June 2026) names no generation. They give different payload figures.
