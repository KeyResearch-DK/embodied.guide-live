Notes

Originally developed at RoMeLa in a joint effort with Westwood Robotics.

On ordering, the buyer gains access to the open-source database with the wiki, CAD files and source code.

As of 19 January 2024, BRUCE can walk out of the box, with some pre-programmed arm motions.

Onboard computer: Khadas Edge 2.
