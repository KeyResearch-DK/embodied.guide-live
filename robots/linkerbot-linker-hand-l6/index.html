Notes

Linkerbot's support FAQ, which answers for its dexterous hands in general, gives “How long does delivery and installation take?” with “7~15 working days.” and “What is the product warranty period?” with “1 year.”

The specification table gives “Max Five-Finger Grasp Force. 50N.” and “Repeat Positioning Accuracy. <±0.2mm.”

The product page gives “a tested service life of over one million cycles” and “A 0.35 s opening / closing response”.

The specification table lists “Static Current. 0.2A.”, “Average No-Load Motion Current. 0.6A.” and “Max Current. 5.6A.”

The product page lists the colors “White, Black, Silver”.
