Notes

  • Unitree's own page presents the H2 series as two product lines, H2-A and H2-D, each with further configurations (H2-A Standard: fixed base; H2-D Standard: manual-move chassis; H2-D Flagship: mobile chassis). The figures given are for H2-D only, and only its Flagship (mobile chassis) configuration, the only one that can move under its own power; H2-A is a separate product line.
  • H2-D Standard (“Manual-Move Chassis”) has no powered locomotion of its own (“/” for maximum mobility speed, drive system and chassis sensors) and must be moved by hand; it is heavier than the Flagship configuration (approx. 148 kg against approx. 120 kg).
  • The Flagship (mobile) base drives on servo motors with dual-wheel differential drive, and carries one LiDAR unit, one depth camera, two physical-collision sensors and two ultrasonic sensors.
  • Rated and peak arm-payload figures are stated per arm (“rated payload of up to 7 kg per arm”); no whole-body payload figure is given.
  • Two onboard computers are fitted (PC1: Intel Core i5 for platform capabilities, PC2: Intel Core i7 for custom development), alongside an optional high-performance computing module “such as Thor”.
