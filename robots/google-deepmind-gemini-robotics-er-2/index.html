Notes

Google DeepMind calls Gemini Robotics ER 2 its most capable embodied reasoning model for robotics; it plans tasks for a robot and hands the movement to a separate vision-language-action model.

Google DeepMind announced the model on 30 July 2026.

Google lists Gemini Robotics ER 1.5 (gemini-robotics-er-1.5-preview) as released on 25 September 2025 and shut down on 30 April 2026, replaced by ER 1.6; and Gemini Robotics ER 1.6 (gemini-robotics-er-1.6-preview) as released on 14 April 2026 and deprecated on 31 August 2026, replaced by Gemini Robotics ER 2.
