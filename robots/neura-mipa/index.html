Notes

The producer's news page of 7 May 2025 is titled “Preview: New MiPA generation presented at OMR”.

The producer showed a preview of its cognitive service robot MiPA at the OMR Festival in Hamburg, calling it a first insight into its next step in cognitive robotics.

The producer says MiPA combines two fine-motor robot arms, autonomous navigation, multimodal sensors and interaction via speech, facial expressions and gestures.

The producer announced the official launch of MiPA for 24-27 June 2025 at Automatica in Munich.

The producer's reservation page speaks of a late 2026 release of MiPA.

The producer says the introductory price applies exclusively to the MiPA Home model for private households.

The producer lists actuated wheels among MiPA's body components.

The producer's datasheet (version V5, 05.06.2026) gives a single-arm payload of 3 kg (7 lbs); this is an arm figure, not what the whole robot can carry.
