Notes

The producer describes Vega as a wheeled humanoid, and Vega U as its upper body for benches and custom bases.

The page says “Vega is in production now, sold as a robot platform for developers and researchers. Order now, or talk to the team.” The Order Now button links to the producer's shop; the page shows no price and no delivery statement.

The page lists the company address as Fremont, CA, US.
