Notes

N2 is a bipedal humanoid with a “Ball Hand” end effector. The specification table lists N2 and N2 EDU.

At the World Humanoid Robot Games, Noetix says its N2 won gold in free gymnastics and silver in solo dance.

Interesting Engineering reported on 15 March 2025 that Noetix Robotics had developed the NOETIX N2, a humanoid able to do continuous backflips, and released footage of it.

Noetix Robotics' After-Sales Terms apply to robots “sold by Noetix Robotics within mainland China” and give N2 a 3-month warranty from initial device activation.
