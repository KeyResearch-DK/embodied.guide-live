Notes

The producer describes Kinisi as a wheeled humanoid mobile manipulator; the specification table marks the autonomous omnidirectional mobile base as “To be confirmed at launch”.

Bear Robotics announced in June 2026 that it would acquire Kinisi Robotics, the Bristol-based startup behind the KR1 robot.

The producer says Bear Robotics was founded in Silicon Valley in 2017 and calls the Bay Area its home.

The page says Kinisi is already sorting glass in live deployments.

A Mera Robotics blog post dates the unveiling of the two-armed KR1 to July 2025.
