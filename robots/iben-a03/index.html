Notes

The producer lists iBen-A03 among its intelligent service robots.

The producer says each arm has five degrees of freedom, with shoulder, elbow and finger movement.

The producer says the arms can salute, shake hands and hug.

The producer says the robot can act as on-site staff and give presentations and introductions to visitors.

The producer's product image shows a head, a torso with a touchscreen and two arms on a closed, round base.

The producer's Chinese site gives its Beijing headquarters at No. 116 Huangchang Road, Chaoyang District (translated from the Chinese).
