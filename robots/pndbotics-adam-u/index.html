Notes

The producer's wiki describes Adam-U as an upper-body robot designed for desktop-level operations and experiments.

The producer's parts drawing shows a head, a torso and two arms on a lift column over a chassis with casters.

The wiki recommends placing a 40 kg counterweight in the chassis to keep the robot balanced.
