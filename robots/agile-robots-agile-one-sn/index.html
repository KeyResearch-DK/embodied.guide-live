Notes

Agile Robots launched Agile ONE SN together with NVIDIA at NVIDIA GTC on 21 October 2026.

The producer's humanoid overview page says Agile ONE SN is Agile ONE S, with Dual Wuji five-finger hands.

Agile ONE SN ships with preconfigured NVIDIA Isaac GR00T software, including the GR00T 1.7 open VLA model and GR00T whole-body control.

The producer gives joint torque of up to 140 Nm and a payload of up to 3 kg per arm. The 3 kg is an arm figure, not what the whole robot can carry.

The producer says Agile ONE SN is scheduled to be available beginning of 2027.

The producer calls Agile ONE its full-size counterpart for industrial applications; skills and data developed on Agile ONE SN can later be transferred to it.

NVIDIA’s press release gives the Agile ONE S humanoid body of Agile ONE SN as 40 kilograms (88 pounds); Agile Robots’ own press release gives 43 kilograms (95 pounds).
