Notes

Galileo showed Galileo X at the 2026 World Robot Conference in Beijing, held 19 to 23 August 2026.

Galileo says the platform breaks the boundaries between AGVs, off-road vehicles and quadruped robots.

In Global mode, Galileo says the platform is adapted to outdoor steps, gullies and gravel.

A trade report on the launch describes four articulated legs, each ending in a wheel.

The same report says 200 kg is the stated mass-production target and that the concept machine carried around 70 kg in the demonstration.

Chinese press describes the machine as switching freely between wheel and leg: wheels on flat ground, legs over rough ground (translated from the Chinese).
