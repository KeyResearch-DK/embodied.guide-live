Notes

ZWHAND's press release of 12 January 2026 from CES presents the lineup as “making a highly anticipated global debut” and lists the “Workhorse A17”: “Featuring 17 active DOFs, it balances dexterity and cost-efficiency.”

ZWHAND's company history lists the A17 under 2024 as the representative of its first-generation dexterous hands (translated from the Chinese).

In a reply to investors on 16 June 2026 the parent company said it had released the dexterous hand products A17, B06 and B20 (translated from the Chinese).

ZWHAND is a brand of Shenzhen Zhaowei Machinery & Electronics Co., Ltd., a maker of micro drives and transmissions (translated from the Chinese).
