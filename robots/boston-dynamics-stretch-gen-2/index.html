Notes

Boston Dynamics announced Stretch Gen 2 on 3 November 2026 and describes it this way: “Boston Dynamics has unveiled Stretch Gen 2, the next generation of its autonomous mobile trailer unloading robot”.

Boston Dynamics first revealed the original Stretch on 29 March 2021.

Boston Dynamics' two Stretch brochures, one for trailer and container unloading and one for case picking, date from February 2026 (the first is marked “Modifed: 02/2026”) and do not mention Gen 2. The trailer-unloading brochure lists “Unloading case rate 600-800 cases per hour” and “Typical charge time to 90% under nominal conditions: 2 hours”. The Gen 2 announcement gives “Stretch Gen 2 unloads up to 1000 cases per hour”, says “Its battery lasts two shifts”, and adds that “a fast charger gets it to 90 percent charge in about 75 minutes”.
