Notes

Promobot calls V.4 a service robot for business.

The robot talks with people, moves around on its own and can be connected to third-party software.

Movement is by two motor-wheels and two steering rollers.

Each arm has 7 degrees of freedom; the producer notes that the true figure is 6 + 1, because the fingers bend only through a limited angle.

The specifications give a power consumption of 400 W.

The producer declares that the robot meets the Customs Union technical regulations on low-voltage equipment (004/2011) and electromagnetic compatibility (020/2011).
