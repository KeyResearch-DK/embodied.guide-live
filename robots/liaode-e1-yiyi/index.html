Notes

China.com calls E1 Yiyi Liaode's flagship product, a full-size walking companion robot with a highly lifelike design (translated from the Chinese).

Liaode showed its range of lifelike robots at WAIC 2026, according to the same report (translated from the Chinese).

It is aimed at family companionship, health and care services, and campus training, according to the same report (translated from the Chinese).

It has a lifelike head with a large language model for conversation in several languages and dialects, according to the same report (translated from the Chinese).

Its upper body can wave, shake hands and hug, according to the same report (translated from the Chinese).

It walks on two legs and can walk straight, turn, turn on the spot and walk backwards, according to the same report (translated from the Chinese).

The robot's name is a pinyin transliteration of the Chinese one.

Liaode says it made a major debut with its full lineup of bionic robots at the 2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference, which kicked off on 17 July 2026.
