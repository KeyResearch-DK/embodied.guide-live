Notes

Reflex Robotics' own website gives no figures in its main text, only general marketing language. The figures (payload, reach, runtime, and the camera/arm description) come from data published elsewhere on the same official page.

Degrees of freedom are given only per body part - a 3-degree-of-freedom head and neck, and 7-degree-of-freedom arms - never as a single total for the whole robot, so no single total number is shown here.

The reach figure the manufacturer gives (6.6 ft / 2 m, also described as “0 to 2+ meter floor-to-shelf reach”) describes how far the arms and lift can extend, not the robot's overall height or footprint - it is kept as a separate note rather than mixed into either measurement.

The country given for the manufacturer (USA) is not stated on Reflex Robotics' own website; business registries (Crunchbase, PitchBook, Built In, CB Insights) agree on New York, USA. The city comes from the company register CB Insights, which gives New York, New York, and from the Brooklyn Navy Yard's tenant directory, which lists Reflex Robotics in Building 77 in Brooklyn.

Reflex has a wheeled base and no legs at all, so it is catalogued here as a wheeled robot rather than a legged or leg-and-wheel-hybrid one.

TechCrunch's article dated “1:35 PM PDT · March 13, 2024” reports that Reflex Robotics was showing its wheeled robot “at Modex in Atlanta this week”.
