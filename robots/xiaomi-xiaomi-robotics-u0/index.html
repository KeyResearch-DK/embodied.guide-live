What the data labels mean

Notes

  • Xiaomi calls Xiaomi-Robotics-U0 a world foundation model trained for embodied intelligence; it generates images and video of robot scenes.
  • Xiaomi presents it as a data engine for robot learning.
  • A separate accelerated version, Xiaomi-Robotics-U0-FlashAR, is released as its own weights; the video checkpoint is not yet released.
