Notes

OpenLoong is an open-source project operated by Humanoid Robot (Shanghai) Co., Ltd.; Qinglong is its full-size public-version machine.

The project publishes Qinglong's hardware design, control framework, whole-body dynamics control software and datasets as open source.

TechNode reported on 5 July 2024 that the National Local Joint Humanoid Robot Innovation Center unveiled Qinglong at WAIC 2024.
