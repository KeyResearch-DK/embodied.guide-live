Notes

Guangzhou International (gz.gov.cn) calls PHYBOT C2 a bipedal humanoid robot that plays badminton with visitors.

It was shown at the 139th Canton Fair, according to the same report.

Qbitai describes C2 as a service robot for exhibition guiding, sports companionship and research and education, with a projector module and an emotion light strip (translated from the Chinese).

36Kr reports that C2 was launched at the opening of PHYBOT's Guangzhou manufacturing centre (translated from the Chinese).
