Notes

A small four-legged robot built for DARPA's Learning Locomotion program, used by university teams including Stanford, CMU, USC and MIT from about 2006 to 2010.

About a dozen identical units, each with a common software API and a motion-capture rig, were distributed to research teams so results could be compared directly.
