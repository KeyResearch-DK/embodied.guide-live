Notes

The page calls Tian Yi 2.0 a wheeled-arm robot suited to patrol and inspection in offices and factory stations.

The page says the humanoid form gives it more general use: the same hardware can take different software modules, for example night patrol and daytime reception.

The page lists remote operation through VR equipment or a cockpit, controlling the chassis and arms; one operator can take over several robots.

The producer's registered name is Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center Co., Ltd. (translated from the Chinese).
