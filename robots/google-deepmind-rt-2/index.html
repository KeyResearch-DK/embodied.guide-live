RT-2

United Kingdom · 2023

Model type
Vision-language-action (VLA)⁠Source and note: Model typeB deepmind.google/blog/rt-2-new-model-translates-vision-and-language-into-action/ · Retrieved 13 October 2026Note. Developer wording: “Robotic Transformer 2 (RT-2) is a novel vision-language-action (VLA) model that learns from both web and robotics data, and translates this knowledge into generalised instructions for robotic control”.
What it controls
Arms and hands⁠Source and note: What it controlsB deepmind.google/blog/rt-2-new-model-translates-vision-and-language-into-action/ · Retrieved 13 October 2026Note. The action is a string of tokens that starts with an end-of-episode flag and then gives “commands to change position and rotation of the end-effector, as well as the desired extension of the robot gripper”.
Inputs
Camera Language⁠Source and note: InputsA robotics-transformer2.github.io · Retrieved 13 October 2026Note. “The resulting model takes in robot camera images and directly predicts actions for a robot to perform.” “The robot data includes the current image, language command and the robot action at the particular time step.”
Base model
PaLI-X or PaLM-E (two versions)⁠Source and note: Base modelB deepmind.google/blog/rt-2-new-model-translates-vision-and-language-into-action/ · Retrieved 13 October 2026Note. “we adapt Pathways Language and Image model (PaLI-X) and Pathways Language model Embodied (PaLM-E) to act as the backbones of RT-2”.

Notes

Google DeepMind introduced RT-2 as a vision-language-action model that learns from web data and robot data.

RT-2 comes in two versions, built on two different vision-language models, PaLM-E and PaLI-X.

Google DeepMind published the RT-2 blog post and paper on 28 July 2023.
