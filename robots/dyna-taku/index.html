Notes

The producer introduced Dyna-2.1, a full-stack physical agent, together with Taku, the semi-humanoid robot at its centre, in a research post.

The research post that introduces Taku is dated September 29, 2026.

The producer describes Taku as a semi-humanoid robot with a human-shaped body above the waist, a folding lower body, a base on four steerable wheels and two 7-degree-of-freedom arms.

The producer says most steps in its target workflows need reach and manipulation precision rather than legs, so Taku moves between stations on wheels, and that it sized Taku like an average person.

The producer says Taku's arms use low-ratio planetary actuators, which allow higher speeds and accelerations than the harmonic drives in conventional arms.

The research post uses a hotel laundry room workflow as its running example; the producer calls commercial laundry one of the deployment verticals it has been focusing on.

The producer says it is based in Redwood City, California.
