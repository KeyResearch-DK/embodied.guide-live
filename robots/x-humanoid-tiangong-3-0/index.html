Notes

X-Humanoid presents Embodied Tiangong 3.0 as its new-generation general-purpose robot platform; the English site navigation writes the name “Embodied Tien Kung3.0”.

The specification image also gives a maximum joint torque of 400 Nm and lists optional dexterous hands.

The producer's registered name is Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center Co., Ltd. (translated from the Chinese).
