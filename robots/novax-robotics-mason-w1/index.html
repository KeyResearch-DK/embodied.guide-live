Notes

The Motek trade fair lists a product novelty dated 8 September 2026 from NovaX Robotics under the name Mason W-01; the producer's own home page spells the name Mason-W1.

The producer's home page describes Mason-W1 as a high performance and durable industrial-grade heavy-duty workhorse unit.

The producer's home page shows Mason-W1 on an omnidirectional wheeled base.

The producer says the omnidirectional wheeled base reaches a maximum speed of 2 m/s with ±2 mm accuracy.
