Notes

Geekplus launched Gino 1 as the world's first general-purpose robot for warehouse scenarios (translated from the Chinese; the release is carried by the Chinese financial wire Cailian).

Geekplus calls Gino 1 a general-purpose humanoid robot built for warehouse operations.

Shanghai Securities News reports that Gino 1 was launched in February 2026 and describes it as Geekplus's first general-purpose two-armed wheeled robot (translated from the Chinese).

Shanghai Securities News reports that Gino 1 had completed internal reliability validation by WAIC 2026 and was expected to enter gradual mass production from the third quarter of 2026 (translated from the Chinese).

Geekplus's offices page lists its corporate headquarters in Shunyi, Beijing.
