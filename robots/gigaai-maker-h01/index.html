Notes

The producer describes Maker H01 as designed for service and home settings, with two arms on a mobile chassis (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's product image shows a head, a torso and two arms on a wheeled base.

The page lists picking and placing items, inspection, reception and helping with experiments in home, industrial, service and research settings (translated from the Chinese).

The producer's about page lists research and operations centres in Beijing, Shanghai, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Shenzhen, Guangzhou and Wuhan (translated from the Chinese).

Company-register records (36Kr PitchHub, CB Insights) place GigaAI in Haidian District, Beijing.

Humanoid.press lists the official unveiling of Maker H01 as November 2025.
