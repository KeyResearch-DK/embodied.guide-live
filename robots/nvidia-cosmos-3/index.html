What the data labels mean

Notes

  • NVIDIA calls Cosmos 3 the first omni-model with native reasoning, world and action generation.
  • NVIDIA also offers Cosmos 3 as a controllable, physics-grounded world simulator.
  • Earlier Cosmos models (Cosmos 2 and 2.5) kept perception and generation as separate models and did not generate actions.
