Notes

Built on the P-series' capabilities, with four in-house algorithms upgraded: high-load stability, multi-modal perception, motion control and upper-mount adaptation.

The manufacturer also gives a folded size of 1150 x 800 x 200 mm, a 595 x 419 x 129 mm cargo bed and a 210 mm wheel diameter.

The manufacturer also gives a module max torque of 450 N·m.
