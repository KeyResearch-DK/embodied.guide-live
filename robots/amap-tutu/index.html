Notes

First batch of Tutu robot dogs delivered to Sands China at NBA House at The Venetian in Macau (press release of 10 October 2026).

Tutu takes on a new role as Sands China's Robotic Guide No. 001.

Per TechNode, Amap debuted Tutu at the 2026 Beijing E-Town Humanoid Robot Half-Marathon.

Per TechNode, Amap says Tutu is designed to guide visually impaired users through complex open environments without preset routes or remote control.

Per TechNode, Amap says Tutu is powered by its in-house ABot technology framework, which combines spatial data, embodied AI models, and agent-based execution.
