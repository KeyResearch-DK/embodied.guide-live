Notes

AgiBot calls GE-Sim 2.0 (Genie Envisioner World Simulator 2.0) a closed-loop video world simulator for robotic manipulation.

AgiBot announced Genie Envisioner 2.0 as the step from World Action Models to interactive world simulators.

The technical report was submitted to arXiv on 26 May 2026.
