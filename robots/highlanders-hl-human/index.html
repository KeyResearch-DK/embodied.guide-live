Notes

Highlanders showed the HL Human prototype for the first time on 30 June 2025 (translated from the Japanese).

In June 2025 the producer said the early prototype would be offered to partner companies from the fourth quarter of 2025 as the HL Human Early Access Program, and that a production model was planned for release in 2026 (translated from the Japanese).

The producer's photos show a head, a torso with two arms and two legs, held up by a stand.

The press release names manufacturing sites, logistics warehouses, infrastructure inspection and disaster support as intended work (translated from the Japanese).

The producer's website lists a humanoid called N in its hardware history.

Mitsubishi Motors and Highlanders signed a memorandum of understanding on 9 July 2026 to examine producing Highlanders' humanoid robots at Mitsubishi's Kyoto plant from early 2027.
