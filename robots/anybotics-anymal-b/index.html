Notes

A research-generation quadruped from ETH Zurich's Robotic Systems Lab, commercialized by ANYbotics; the version described in Hutter et al.'s 2016/2017 papers, predating the commercial ANYmal C.

Torque-controlled series-elastic actuators (ANYdrive) at three joints per leg, sealed to IP67 for oil-and-gas industrial inspection.
