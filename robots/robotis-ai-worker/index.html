Notes

  • The producer calls AI Worker a semi-humanoid robot with a swerve-drive mobile base, two 7-DOF arms and grippers or dexterous hands.
  • The figures are for the FFW-SG2 model. The FFW-BG2 model has no mobile base, runs on mains power, has 19 DOF and weighs 85 kg; the producer says it is sold in Korea only.
  • The producer says the source code, simulation models and training data for AI Worker are published as open source.
  • The producer gives its headquarters address as Magokjungang 5-ro 1-Gil, Gangseo-gu, Seoul.
