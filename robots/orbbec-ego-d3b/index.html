Notes

Orbbec describes EGO-D3B as a depth-enabled head-mounted embodiment-free data acquisition device integrating a Gemini 335L stereo depth camera with RGB, depth, and IMU streams for robot fine manipulation learning.

Intended use, per the manufacturer. “Suitable for household operations, industrial assembly, warehouse picking, tool use, and fine manipulation tasks.”
