Notes

The manual's revision history starts with V1.0.0, dated 2025.9.15, marked as the initial version: “Date 2025.9.15 2026.2.6 2026.4.23 2026.9.8 Revision V1.0.0 V1.0.1 V1.0.2 V1.0.3 Modified content Initial version.” OYMotion's download page lists the ROH-AP002 user manual dated 2026/01/06: “ROH-AP002 DEXTEROUS HANDUSER MANUAL 2026/01/06”.

Available for the left and right hand; versions with the suffix -C have a CAN interface.

The manual states “12-month warranty period from the date of sale.” The packing list includes a warranty card: “Warranty card: 1 piece”.

OYMotion's industrial-solutions article dated 2026.04.22 names the AP002 among its mature series.

The product page gives a repeat positioning accuracy of ±0.05 mm and a force resolution of 0.1 N, and a full-range bending or stretching time of 0.7 s at maximum speed.

The product page says: “The OYMotion ROH-AP002 adopts a hybrid 3D + 1D force sensing architecture. The distal and middle segments of the fingers support both normal and tangential force sensing, while the palm uses an 11×5 dot-matrix sensor.”

The product page gives typical operating power of at most 15 W and standby power of at most 0.5 W.

The manual warns: “Warning: ROH-AP002 dexterous hand is not waterproof.”
