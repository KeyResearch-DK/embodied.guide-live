Notes

The producer's Kargo page lists two seven-axis robot arms, two vision cameras, one lifting module and one mobile platform.

The producer's Kargo page describes a differential mobile platform with waist lift and pitch.

The producer's parameter table gives a waist lift range of 0 to 780 mm, a waist range of 0° to 90°, a neck rotation range of ±90° and a neck pitching range of -20° to 45°.

Shanghai Securities News, reporting from JAKA on 11 October 2026, says the heavy-load JAKA Kargo50 belongs to JAKA's Kargo series of wheeled humanoid robots (translated from the Chinese).

The same report says the Kargo10 model in that series has been deployed commercially at scale (translated from the Chinese).
