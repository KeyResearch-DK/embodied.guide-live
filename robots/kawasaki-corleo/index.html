Notes

A rideable, hydrogen-powered quadruped from Kawasaki Heavy Industries' Safe Adventure Business Development Team.

A 150cc hydrogen engine generator powers electric drive units at each leg, controlled by weight-shift sensors in stirrups and handlebars.
