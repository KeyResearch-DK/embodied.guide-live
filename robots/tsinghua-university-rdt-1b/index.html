The developer’s own claims2/2 disclosed

Learns new tasksNeeds retraining
Source and note: Learns new tasksB rdt-robotics.github.io/rdt-robotics/ · Retrieved 13 October 2026Note. Developer claim: “The following videos show that RDT can learn new skills with 1-5 demonstrations.” The demonstrations are part of the fine-tuning data: “We now list the number of demos related to each task in our fine-tuning dataset”. For a new robot platform the model card advises: “we recommend collecting a small dataset of the target robot and then using it to fine-tune RDT”.
Works across robotsYes
Source and note: Works across robotsA huggingface.co/robotics-diffusion-transformer/rdt-1b · Retrieved 13 October 2026Note. Developer claim: “RDT is compatible with almost all modern mobile manipulators, from single-arm to dual-arm”. The same card lists a limit: “RDT cannot yet generalize to new robot platforms (not seen in the pre-training datasets)”.Yes only if the developer itself says the model runs on different robot models, for example robots from two manufacturers or two of one manufacturer’s models. Several units of the same robot do not count.

Notes

The model card names its developers as the RDT team, researchers from the TSAIL group at Tsinghua University.

The RDT-1B paper was first submitted to arXiv on 10 October 2024.

The Tsinghua University contact page gives its address as Haidian District, Beijing.
