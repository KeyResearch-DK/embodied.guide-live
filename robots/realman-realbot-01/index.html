Notes

The producer describes the RealBot-01 as a wheeled humanoid robot with two RX75 arms (translated from the Chinese).

The wheeled chassis and the humanoid upper body can be separated. The upper body holds the head, both arms and the torso, and can be fitted to other chassis models (translated from the Chinese).

The producer lists a standard version with an Intel RealSense D405 and a data-collection version with an Orbbec 335LG as the standard camera (translated from the Chinese).

The company history lists the founding of Realman Intelligent Technology (Beijing) in 2018 (translated from the Chinese).
