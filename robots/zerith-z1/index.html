Notes

The producer's own site shows only the H1 series.

A CMRA report says Zerith Technology launched the Z1, its first bipedal humanoid robot, on October 24, 2024.

The same CMRA report on the October 2024 launch gives 27 degrees of freedom and joint motors with a peak torque of 150 Nm.

A Hefei government press-office article visits the maker, Hefei Zerith Robotics Co., Ltd., in the China Vision industrial park (translated from the Chinese).

According to the CEO in that article, the robot can already be applied widely in education and entertainment and in settings such as kitchens, exhibition halls and hotels (translated from the Chinese).
