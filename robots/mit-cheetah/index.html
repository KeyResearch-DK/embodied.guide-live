Notes

MIT's first electrically-actuated running quadruped, using custom high-torque motors in the shoulders instead of hydraulics, with a flexible polyurethane-and-Kevlar spine.

Its running efficiency was reported as competitive with running animals, and more efficient than Boston Dynamics' BigDog or Honda's ASIMO.
