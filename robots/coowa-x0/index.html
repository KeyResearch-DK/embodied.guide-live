Notes

The producer says X0 is built on its Aether-Magnum heavy-duty quadruped platform, with an integrated self-developed 7-DoF arm (translated from the Chinese).

The producer lists the arm's working height as 0 to 150 cm (up to 170 cm), reach of ±75 cm (up to ±100 cm) and maximum arm-end load of 5 kg (translated from the Chinese). It is an arm figure, not a whole-robot payload.

36Kr reports that X0, the company's first heavy-duty quadruped for complex urban scenes, made its first offline appearance at WAIC 2026 in Shanghai (translated from the Chinese).

36Kr reports that X0 has completed multi-scenario validation and entered real-machine deployment for fine-grained urban maintenance (translated from the Chinese).

36Kr describes 68 kg as X0's heavy-load capability (translated from the Chinese), while the producer's specification table lists 68 kg as the whole-machine mass.

The producer's website lists offices in Shanghai, Beijing and Shenzhen (translated from the Chinese).
