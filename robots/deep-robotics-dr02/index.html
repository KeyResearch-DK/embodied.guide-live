Notes

DEEP Robotics markets DR02 as “the world's first humanoid robot with an IP66 protection rating capable of all-weather outdoor operations” - a full-body waterproof and dustproof design aimed at industrial use in rain, humidity and dust.

Stated operating temperature range: -20 degrees C to 55 degrees C, described as enabling continuous operation from cold-storage facilities to high-temperature workshops.

The page describes a modular quick-detach and quick-replace design for core components (forearms, full arms, full legs), aimed at reducing maintenance downtime; no further technical detail or figures are given for this feature.

The site's navigation also lists a “Humanoid Robot DR01” next to DR02, but the DR02 page itself never states that DR01 is its predecessor, so no predecessor link is made here.

No weight, height, degrees of freedom, payload, top speed, slope rating, obstacle/step height, battery capacity, runtime, onboard compute, ROS2/SDK support, or certifications are stated anywhere on the producer's own page; only a demo video and the marketing text above are given.

DEEP Robotics' press release of 10 October 2025 (Newsfile) says it officially launched DR02 on 9 October 2025.
