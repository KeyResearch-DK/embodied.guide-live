Notes

The page describes H10-W as an assistant robot for research, education, commercial and consumer use, built from a wheeled base, two 7-DoF arms and a linearly lifting torso, that picks up, carries and places objects on spoken commands.

The torso lifts at up to 300 mm/s.

The mobile base uses two-wheel differential drive.

The model is listed on the Chinese website of Agile Robots, whose footer gives the company's Munich address first and a Beijing office second.
