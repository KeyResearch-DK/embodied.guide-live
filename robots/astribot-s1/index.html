Notes

Astribot's own product page (astribot.com/en/product) gives specifications only as a comparison table against a “Normal Adult Male” column; no chassis, wheel or leg description appears in the page text. Astribot's own homepage (astribot.com/en) hosts a product video labelled “1x speed | No teleoperation | Real shot” and “Astribot S1”; the video's poster image (https://www.astribot.com/index/s2testen/index-s2-test-en.webp) shows the full robot standing on a rectangular base with a visible caster wheel at each corner and no legs — classified as humanoid_wheeled on this producer-supplied image.

The page states single-arm DoF as 7 and gives no whole-body degrees-of-freedom total; the per-arm figure alone is not treated as a whole-body count.

The 5kg payload figure is a single-arm figure at full horizontal reach, not a whole-body payload.

The effector's “≥10 m/s” maximum velocity and “≈100 m/s²” acceleration are arm/end-effector figures, not a body locomotion speed.

Endurance is given as “4~6 hours, supports plug-in”; the “supports plug-in” wording is ambiguous between mains-tethered operation and a swappable battery, so hot-swap is unconfirmed.
