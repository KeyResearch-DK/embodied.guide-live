Notes

Hello Robot's datasheet describes Stretch 4 as “a highly capable, 9-DOF platform designed for indoor commercial and industrial environments”.

Hello Robot announced Stretch 4 on its forum on 12 May 2026: “Today we are thrilled to announce Stretch 4”. Its purchase page states: “Stretch 4 typically ships within 2-4 weeks from receipt of purchase order or pre-payment.”

The purchase page's FAQ states: “Stretch 4 is currently only certified for laboratory and research use. We are working towards certifications.”
