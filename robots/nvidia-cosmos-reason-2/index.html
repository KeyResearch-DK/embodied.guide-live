What the data labels mean

Notes

  • NVIDIA calls Cosmos Reason 2 an open reasoning vision language model for physical AI and robotics that can serve as a planning model for an embodied agent.
  • NVIDIA released the Cosmos-Reason2 models on 19 December 2025; the 32B size followed on Hugging Face on 29 April 2026.
  • NVIDIA now points users to Cosmos 3 and keeps this repository on limited maintenance; the Cosmos Reason 2 weights stay on Hugging Face.
  • NVIDIA Corporation lists its address as Santa Clara, California.
