Several units of the same robot do not count.

Notes

The project page and the paper list Galbot, Peking University, The University of Hong Kong and Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence as institutions, with Galbot first. The project page and the code repository are hosted under the PKU-EPIC organisation. The paper abstract says: “GraspVLA, a VLA model pretrained on large-scale synthetic action data as a foundational model for grasping tasks”.
