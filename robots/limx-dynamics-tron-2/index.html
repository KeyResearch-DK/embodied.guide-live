Notes

The producer's company history says it launched TRON 2 in December 2025.

The producer calls TRON 2 a multi-form embodied robot that can be configured as dual-arm, bipedal or wheeled.

The producer says the dual-wheeled-leg form supports 30 kg payloads; the figure is for that form only.

The producer says the arm has 7 degrees of freedom and a 73 cm reach; the figure is for the dual-arm form.

The producer says the dual-arm form has a 10 kg dual-arm payload; the figure is for that form only.
