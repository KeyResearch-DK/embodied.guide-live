Notes

The robot is sold by Flourish Robots Corporation, with an address in Dover, Delaware, USA.

The producer says shipping starts in December 2026 and that the first 50 units are numbered 01 to 50.

Tasks are taught by guiding the robot through them once from a phone; the producer says this takes 30 minutes and no code.

The producer's photo shows a head, a torso and two arms on a column above a round base; the page names neither wheels nor legs.
