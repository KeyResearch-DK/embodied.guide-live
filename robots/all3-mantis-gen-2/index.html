Notes

Unveiled in a press release dated 7 October 2026.

Weight 135 kg, and 180 kg with its arm, per the head of mobile robotics.

A six-axis arm with a reach of around 1.8 m currently carries a screw tool or a vacuum gripper.

A rotating yaw hip gives two stances: wide for working and narrow to pass a standard 900 mm doorway.

Next milestone: deployment on its first live construction project in early 2027.
