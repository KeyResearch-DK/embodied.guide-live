Notes

Haoqizhe is the pinyin of the Chinese product name. The manufacturer launched the S01 at WAIC 2026 in Shanghai and calls it an industrial high-speed wheeled quadruped robot (translated from the Chinese).

The manufacturer says the S01 brings the four-protection design of the MOVENEW P series to a small-to-medium body for narrow spaces such as substation interlayers and pipe galleries (translated from the Chinese).

A navigation article on the manufacturer's site shows the S01 carrying MIC Navi software for autonomous patrol. It says the code runs on the robot's main board, with no extra compute kit (translated from the Chinese).
