Notes

A ground-combat quadruped swarm system with a command vehicle plus reconnaissance, strike and support 'wolf' units, first shown at Airshow China (Zhuhai), November 2024.

Built by Jianshe Industry (Group) Co., a Chongqing-based former arsenal now under China South Industries Group, pursuing swarm-combat rather than single-unit 'robot dog' concepts.
