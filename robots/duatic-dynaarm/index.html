Notes

Duatic describes the arm on its DynaArm page: “The DynaArm is an ultra-lightweight yet powerful robotic arm, designed for dynamic applications like mobile robots.”

Duatic's team announced the arm on the ROS Discourse forum on 21 December 2024: “We are thrilled to announce the completion of our first DynaArm”, a fully ROS 2-controlled robotic arm.

The DynaArm page lists a “Power System 48V (DC)” and states “No external control box required”.
