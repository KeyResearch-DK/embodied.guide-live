Notes

Most of Memo's technical figures come from a dedicated specifications page on Sunday Robotics' own website, separate from the homepage.

Degrees of freedom are given only as a breakdown by body part - arms 2x7, hands 2x4, torso 1, lower body 4 - never as a single total for the whole robot, so no single total number is shown here.

Ingress protection (dust/water resistance) is given per body part only - the hand is rated IP67, the lower arm IP66 - never as one rating for the whole robot, and no rating at all is given for the torso or head.

The reach figures the manufacturer gives (0.8 m horizontal, 2.1 m vertical) describe how far the arms can extend, not the robot's overall height or footprint - they are kept as a separate note rather than mixed into either measurement.

The homepage gives a manufacturing cost (“Building a single Memo today... costs approximately $20,000”) and explicitly says it is not the retail price (“We don't expect that to be the retail price... exact pricing will be announced closer to launch”).

The country given for the manufacturer (USA) comes from Sunday Robotics' own website: a co-founder's biography places the team in Silicon Valley, California. The city comes from the producer's careers page, which lists its on-site positions in Redwood City, California, and from LinkedIn, which gives Redwood City as the headquarters. The terms of use name the courts in Mountain View as venue, which is not an address.

Memo has a wheeled base and no legs at all, so it is catalogued here as a wheeled robot rather than a legged or leg-and-wheel-hybrid one.
