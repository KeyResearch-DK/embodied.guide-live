Notes

The producer calls Voxel a droid butler: “Voxel is a droid butler with a single goal: to maintain your home to the highest standard.”

The producer describes the hardware shown so far as a prototype: “The Voxel shown in our launch materials, including videos, images, demonstrations and prototypes, is a concept and development prototype.” It adds: “We expect to substantially redesign Voxel’s hardware before delivery.”

The producer says: “Deliveries are expected to begin in 2028 and may be later.”

Leg design: “Z-Shaped Legs with Impact-Aware Feet”. “Articulated leg architecture allows for close approach to countertops, tables, sectionals, and shelving.”

Tuesday Labs describes itself as “a New York City robotics lab and design studio”.

The Gadgeteer article is dated “November 3, 2026” and says “you can reserve a Series-1 now”.
