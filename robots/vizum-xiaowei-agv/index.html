Notes

The WRC exhibitor page gives the company's English name as Vizum and says it was founded in 2016 (translated from the Chinese).

The producer describes the Xiaowei AGV version as having a highly human-like upper body on a wheeled base that supports automatic navigation, and a body that can be raised and lowered (translated from the Chinese).

The producer says the robot can act as a helper in industry, home, commercial, education and research settings (translated from the Chinese).
