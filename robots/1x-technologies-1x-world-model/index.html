What the data labels mean

Notes

  • 1X Technologies describes 1XWM as a video-pretrained world model that is integrated into NEO as a robot policy: it generates video of the intended future from a text prompt and a starting frame, and a separate model turns that video into robot actions.
  • The name 1X World Model is older: 1X first showed a world model that works as a virtual simulator for its robots on 17 September 2024, and described the 1XWM evaluation use on 16 June 2025; the policy use was published on 12 January 2026.
